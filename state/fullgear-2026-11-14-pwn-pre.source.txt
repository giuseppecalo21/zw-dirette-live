AEW Collision’s Full Gear “Tailgate Brawl” Pre-Show

Phoenix, Arizona, at Mortgage Matchup Center

Simulcast live November 14, 2026, on TNT and HBO Max

Excalibur, Walker Stewart, and Tony Schiavone checked in from the broadcast table… A video package spotlighted the AEW World Championship match that will headline the pay-per-view… Ring announcer Arkady Aura delivered the introductions while the entrances for the opening match took place…

1. Jack Perry vs. The Beast Mortos. Perry sent Mortos to the floor and followed. Mortos pressed Perry over his head and dropped him on the apron. Mortos whipped Perry toward the ring steps, but Perry disappeared underneath the ring. Perry reemerged and then dropkicked Mortos. Perry walked the ringside barricade and performed a hurcanrana on the floor.

Back in the ring, Mortos popped up Perry and performed a Samoan Drop before a picture-in-picture break. [C] Mortos performed a backbreaker. When Perry stood up, he put him right back down with a lariat and then covered him for a near fall. Perry battled back and performed a piledriver and then followed up with a knee strike.

Perry sat Mortos on a top turnbuckle. Perry leaned through the ropes, looked into the camera, and said, “Samoa Joe, this is for you, you son of a bitch.” Perry spat at the camera and then performed a Muscle Buster on Mortos before pinning him.

Jack Perry beat The Beast Mortos in 9:25.

Afterward, a fan passed Perry a cup of beer. Perry took a swig of the beer and handed it back to the fan. Perry pulled a young girl out of the front row. Perry stood the girl on the barricade and then pointed at her, which drew cheers from the live crowd…

A brief video package aired on the AEW National Championship match scheduled for the pay-per-view…. [C]

Powell’s POV: A good pre-show opener, and it didn’t overstay its welcome. The fan-friendly Perry with the young girl is great. Tough guy Perry spitting at the camera and calling Joe a son of a bitch is an eye-roller.

A graphic listed the location as the Utah Salt Flats and noted that AEW cameras were sent to document “a private meeting” between the Death Riders and the Conglomeration. Jon Moxley and Marina Shafir waited by their vehicle when Orange Cassidy, Roderick Strong, and Kyle O’Reilly exited the vehicle.

Moxley tossed a backpack to Cassidy, who did not attempt to catch it. Strong and O’Reilly picked up the backpack and looked inside. O’Reilly said it didn’t make them trust Moxley, who said he didn’t ask him to. Moxley asked why they would after all the ugliness between them and the uncomfortable decisions that were made. Moxley extended his hand and said he did not apologize by rule. Cassidy approached Moxley, removed his shades, and said he didn’t ask Moxley to. Cassidy put his shades on and walked away without shaking Moxley’s hand…

2. Kevin Knight, Wardlow, and El Clon (w/Josh Alexander, Lance Archer, Rocky Romero, Trent Beretta) vs. Jordan Cruz, Jaden Monroe, and RJ Wylde. Knight won the quick squash match after hitting UFO Splash on Cruz.

Kevin Knight, Wardlow, and El Clon beat Jordan Cruz, Jaden Monroe, and RJ Wylde in 2:05.

Afterward, Romero got a microphone and said Kyle Fletcher would bring the AEW World Championship home to the Don Callis Family. Trent Beretta called out their opponents, who made their entrances…

3. Big Boom AJ, QT Marshall, Matt Sydal, and Darius Martin (w/Christopher Daniels) vs. Rocky Romero, Trent Beretta, Josh Alexander, and Lance Archer. Sydal got a near fall on Beretta before an early PIP break. [C] Beretta and Romero performed a double stomp and Deadeye combo move on Marshall and had him beat, but AJ broke it up.

The babyface team came back. Darius ran the ropes and went for a suicide dive on Archer, but he didn’t even come close. In the ring, Sydal hit Romero with a Shooting Star Press, and then legal man Martin returned to the ring and covered him for the win…

Big Boom AJ, QT Marshall, Matt Sydal, and Darius Martin beat Rocky Romero, Trent Beretta, Josh Alexander, and Lance Archer in roughly 10:00.

A graphic listed the TBS Championship match as coming up next… [C]

Powell’s POV: Is the goal of the pre-show to sell the pay-per-view or get a bunch of people on the show?

Entrances for the TBS Championship match took place. Excalibur said Maya World was unable to appear due to travel issues, so Tony Khan decided to make the match a four-way…

4. Queen Aminata vs. Persephone (w/Britt Baker) vs. Hyan vs. Skye Blue in a four-way for the TBS Championship. Baker sat in on commentary. She told Schiavone not to piss her off because she was dehydrated, bloated, and had just arrived today due to travel issues.

Aminata and Hyan suplexed their opponents simultaneously, then got up and threw simultaneous clotheslines, leaving all four wrestlers down before a PIP break. [C]
