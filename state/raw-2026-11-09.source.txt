WWE Raw Results
November 9, 2026
Greenville, South Carolina (Bon Secours Wellness Arena)
Commentary: Joe Tessitore and Wade Barrett
Results by: Mike Tedesco of Wrestleview.com

“Then. Now. Forever. Together.” The WWE video leads us into the show.

The WWE Drone flies around the exterior of the Bon Secours Wellness Arena.

Raw Open: Highlights from this past Saturday’s WWE Crown Jewel event are shown.

Pre-Show Arrivals

World Heavyweight Champion Roman Reigns, Jade Cargill, WWE Women’s Tag Team Champions B-Fab and Michin, WWE Intercontinental Champion “All Ego” Ethan Page, and “The American Nightmare” Cody Rhodes are shown arriving for WWE Raw.

World Tag Team Champions Je’Von Evans and Penta kick off WWE Raw

World Tag Team Champions Je’Von Evans and Penta make their entrance to the ring. They defeated Bron Breakker and Austin Theory to win the World Tag Team Championship this past Saturday at WWE Crown Jewel. Penta also holds the Money in the Bank briefcase.

Penta and Evans get the crowd going. Penta thanks the crowd and says they love them. Penta shouts that he and Evans are the new World Tag Team Champions. Evans apologizes and says he doesn’t mean to be selfish, but this is his first WWE Title of his career. If you’re proud or excited for the Young OG or Penta, they can say, “YURR.” The crowd says it lightly, so Evans gets them to do it again. A “You Deserve It” chant picks up. Penta says they are standing here as the new champions. The most important thing is that Penta has zero miedo. Penta and Evans embrace in the ring.

All of a sudden, Bron Breakker viciously hits Penta with a Spear! Breakker then hits a military press gutbuster on Evans before tossing him out of the ring. The crowd boos Breakker. Breakker says there is one man he has to fight, and he’s not leaving until they do. Breakker calls Oba Femi out. The crowd starts to chant for Femi.

“The Ruler” Oba Femi makes his entrance to a great ovation, and he’s dressed to compete! Femi charges into the ring, attacks Breakker, and hits him with a clothesline over the top rope! Femi mentions that Breakker said tonight in Greenville. Breakker has got it!

Simple opener with Penta and Je'Von Evans celebrating before Bron Breakker attacked them.

The bigger news is that Bron Breakker is taking on Oba Femi right now! A match that honestly should be held off for an eventual PLE main event far down the line is happening on #WWERaw .

-Commercial Break-

Bron Breakker vs. “The Ruler” Oba Femi

Referee: Rod Zapata

The bell rings, and Breakker quickly attacks Femi before shouldering him in the midsection in the corner. Femi fights back and whips him hard into the opposite corner before connecting with a clothesline. Femi pulls him up, but Breakker powers him into the corner and shoulders him in the midsection. The referee backs Breakker up, and Femi connects with a STIFF straight right hand to the jaw. Femi scoops Breakker up and hits snake eyes. Femi elbows Breakker in the head and wrenches his arm. Femi shoulders him down before scooping him back up. Breakker slides off, ducks a pair of clotheslines, and hits a big clothesline to take The Ruler down. Logan Paul , Austin Theory , and Maxxine Dupri are at ringside. Breakker has Femi cornered and is attacking him. Femi reverses a whip and hits an Irish Curse Backbreaker. Femi mounts Breakker and punches him. Femi hits Breakker with a shoulderbreaker. Breakker gets out of the ring to recover.

Femi exits the ring, and Breakker tackles him into the ring apron. Femi fires back by sending Breakker into the ring post. Femi goes for a running back elbow, but Breakker drives him into the ring post! Breakker bounces Femi off the commentary table and goes to the apron. Breakker dives off with a clothesline over the table! Joe Tessitore way oversells the call.

-Commercial Break-

Picture-in-Picture Commercial Break: After a few moments taken to recover, Breakker attacks Femi before getting him into the ring. Breakker punches him in the corner and taunts the crowd. Breakker whips Femi hard into the corner shoulder-first. Breakker hits Femi with a vertical suplex for a two-count. Breakker applies a front facelock. Femi fights up to his feet and applies a bear hug, but Breakker fights out. Femi whips Breakker into the corner and hits a running back elbow. Femi tries again, but Breakker moves. Breakker punches Femi and taunts the crowd.

Back from the break, Breakker mocks Femi’s strut. Breakker goes for a vertical suplex, but Femi blocks it. Breakker fights through and hits Femi with a vertical suplex for a two-count. Breakker sets up for another suplex, but Femi fires back with a vertical release suplex.

After a moment to recover, Femi hits Breakker with a pop-up uppercut. Femi hits a pair of running back elbows in the corner before dropping Breakker with a leaping back elbow. Breakker rolls out of the ring, so Femi follows him out and hits a leaping back elbow over the commentary table! Femi stands on the table and holds his arm in pain. Femi gets Breakker into the ring. Breakker distracts the referee as Theory grabs Femi. Femi easily kicks Theory away and gets into the ring. Breakker goes for a Spear, but Femi drives him into the ring post. Femi hits a massive Chokeslam and covers. 1… 2… Breakker kicks out.

Femi gets to his feet and holds his injured shoulder as he signals for another Chokeslam. Breakker gets out, so Femi big boots him. Breakker boots him back, but Femi hits a running uppercut. Breakker hits the ropes and hits Femi with a Torpedo Spear! 1… 2… Femi kicks out. The referee checks on Femi as Logan Paul slides a pair of brass knuckles to Breakker. Breakker puts them on. Femi ducks a loaded right hand and hits a release slam on Breakker. Paul quickly jumps onto the apron to distract the referee. Femi goes for the Fall from Grace, but Breakker punches him in the forehead with the brass knuckles! Femi collapses. Breakker pulls his straps down and hits the ropes for a Torpedo Spear, but Femi stuns him with a Fall from Grace! Theory and Paul quickly get into the ring and double-team Femi.

Winner by Disqualification: “The Ruler” Oba Femi

World Tag Team Champions Penta and Je’Von Evans run down for the save, but The Vision quickly escapes before any contact is made.
