By Jason Powell , ProWrestling.net Editor ( @prowrestlingnet )

AEW Full Gear

Phoenix, Arizona, at Mortgage Matchup Center

Aired live on November 14, 2026, on pay-per-view

AEW Full Gear pre-show results: Jack Perry beat The Beast Mortos in 9:25, Kevin Knight, Wardlow, and El Clon beat Jordan Cruz, Jaden Monroe, and RJ Wylde in 2:05, Big Boom AJ, QT Marshall, Matt Sydal, and Darius Martin beat Rocky Romero, Trent Beretta, Josh Alexander, and Lance Archer in roughly 10:00, and Queen Aminata defeated Persephone, Hyan, and Skye Blue in a four-way in 10:55 to retain the TBS Championship…

The show opened with a Full Gear video package… Excalibur, Nigel McGuinness, and Tony Schiavone were on commentary. Excalibur said they would be on the call for the entire night…

A video package set up the AEW International Championship match… Adam Copeland made his entrnace while ring announcer Arkady Aura introduced him. Yes, the fans sang his entrance theme after it stopped playing. Kazuchika Okada made his entrance with Don Callis…

1. Kazuchika Okada (w/Don Callis) vs. Adam Copeland for the AEW International Championship. The fans cheered loudly before the opening bell. Okada and Copeland met in the middle of the ring and looked at each other, then at the cheering fans, who broke into an “AEW” chant followed by a “F— Don Callis” chant.

Okada went for an early lariat that Copeland avoided. Copeland charged for a spear, but Okada rolled out of the ring to avoid it. Okada ran Copeland into the ring steps. Okada pulled up a floor mat to expose the concrete. Okada set up Copeland for a move, but he reversed it by backdropping him onto the floor mat.

Copeland set up a chair and then atomic dropped Okada onto it before booting him in the face. Copeland maintained control until he decided to turn his attention to Callis. Okada took advantage of the distraction by dropkicking Copeland. Back in the ring, Okada put Copeland down with a neckbreaker.
