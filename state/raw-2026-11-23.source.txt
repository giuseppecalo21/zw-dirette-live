WWE Raw Results
November 23, 2026
Wichita, Kansas (Intrust Bank Arena)
Commentary: Michael Cole and Corey Graves
Results by: Mike Tedesco of Wrestleview.com

“Then. Now. Forever. Together.” The WWE video leads us into the show.

We go live from Intrust Bank Arena to see The 946 viciously beating the Alpha Academy . The Alpha Academy had just finished a match that was taped for WWE Main Event when The 946 attacked. Cruz Montana gives Akira Tozawa a back suplex onto the commentary table. Bronco Nima and Lucien Price give Otis a double-team Samoan Drop. Carmelo Hayes grabs a microphone and says the Alpha Academy are trash.

Royce Keys sits uninterestedly in the ring as The 946 gather around him. Hayes says The Bloodline is watching what’s happening as they sit on Roman Reigns’ bus. That’s the difference between them. The Bloodline thrives on comfort, but The 946 thrives on adversity and struggle. That’ll always be the difference. The crowd starts to loudly boo. Hayes says no one has ever challenged The Bloodline like them. They’ve bled all over the place. There are sharks and piranhas. They want everything on The Bloodline’s plate. At WarGames, they’ll realize that blood isn’t thicker than mud. At Survivor Series, The Bloodline will acknowledge them.

Nima says they’re more than just a family. The same pressures that bend and break you are the same ones they carry on their shoulders. No one feels The 946. Price says you’re either with them or against them. An “OTC” chant fires up. Price says they’re here to get paid, so you either lay down or get down because today’s price isn’t yesterday’s price.

Montana says some of them have fought for their lives for the opportunity they have today. Now that they have it in their grasp, there’s no one alive who can take it from them. They’ve gone through life having to make something from nothing, so they’ve got to squeeze it for everything it’s got. They’re at war. At Survivor Series, The Bloodline will pay in blood.

Another “OTC” chant fires up. The 946 gathers around Keys. Keys says Roman Reigns is a rich, wealthy man. He’s the Big Dog, the Tribal Chief, and the OTC. All this started because of what he told Jacob Fatu to do to him. What’d Reigns tell him? That Keys isn’t family, blood, or Bloodline. He’s just one of the boys. Keys says he’s got his boys now. People like them jump into the deep end and swim with the sharks. At Survivor Series, they’ll take out Reigns’ Bloodline. Then, Keys is coming for the grand prize. He’s coming after Reigns. Jaida Parker takes the microphone and says, “Ain’t nobody safe.”

This Saturday, The 946 battles The Bloodline in a WarGames Match at Survivor Series.

Strong start to #WWERaw . I like when they start with some chaos to pull you in quickly. The show is so formulaic that whenever they break the flow even a little bit, it feels very fresh. Great stuff to keep building The 946. I'm interested to see how #SurvivorSeries turns out.

Pre-Show Arrivals

Stephanie Vaquer and Sol Ruca are shown arriving.

WWE Intercontinental Champion “All Ego” Ethan Page makes his entrance. He puts the title on the line against AAA World Cruiserweight Champion Rey Fénix next.

-Commercial Break-

WWE Intercontinental Championship 
“All Ego” Ethan Page (c) vs. AAA World Cruiserweight Champion Rey Fénix

Referee: Rod Zapata

Fénix ducks a lock-up and hooks a side headlock. Page whips him off and shoulder tackles him down before posing. Fénix kips up and gets in his face. Page sends him into the ropes, but Fénix ducks a clothesline and hits a running hurricanrana, followed by a dropkick that sends him out of the ring. As Page gets back into the ring, Fénix hits him with a rolling thunder hurricanrana for a near fall! Page rolls out of the ring and avoids a baseball slide. Page attacks Fénix as he slides into the ring and punches away at his skull. Page sends Fénix into the ropes for a back elbow to the jaw. Fénix punches Page as he approaches him. Page quickly knees him in the midsection to take him down. Page hits a backbreaker and takes a moment to collect himself. Fénix chops Page, but Page kicks him in the ribs. Fénix may have injured his wrist blocking the kick. Page lifts Fénix, but Fénix slides off and rocks him with a pair of superkicks. Page is stunned against the ropes. Fénix goes to the top rope for Adios, Amigo, but Page shakes the ropes, knocking him to the floor!

-Commercial Break-

Picture-in-Picture Commercial Break: Page keeps the advantage over Fénix, driving him into the barricade and the ring apron before getting him into the ring. Fénix responds with some chops and rebounds off the ropes before stunning Page with a superkick. Fénix backs up, gets the crowd going, then charges in for a double knee strike to the face. Both men are down. Fénix is still holding his wrist in pain. Page forearms Fénix in the jaw and chops him in the corner. Page puts Fénix on the top rope and climbs to the second rope. Page attacks Fénix and taunts the crowd.

Fénix elbows away at Page to avoid a slam off the top rope. Fénix then hits a wild super hurricanrana and follows up with a massive low-down frog splash! 1… 2… Page kicks out.

Fénix grabs Page, shakes off a strike, and superkicks him. Fénix goes for a handspring cutter, but Page sidesteps it before hitting a powerslam for a near fall. Page sets up for an Ego-Plex, but Fénix counters into an inside cradle for a near fall. Page pops up and immediately connects with a big boot for a two-count.

The crowd is chanting for Fénix. Page puts Fénix on the top rope, but Fénix slips down and hits him with a powerbomb off the second rope. Fénix sets up for a rolling cutter, but Page superkicks him. Page pushes Fénix into the ropes, but he hits a handspring cutter. Fénix goes to the corner and hits a rolling thunder cutter for a near fall!

Michael Cole says this is an incredible match to kick off Friday Night SmackDown. Oops. Page rolls out of the ring, so Fénix CRUSHES him with a suicide dive somersault plancha. Fénix gets Page into the ring and goes to the top rope, but Page rolls to the floor. Fénix spins and hits a BEAUTIFUL moonsault block to the floor! Fénix gets Page in the ring and hits a running kick in the corner. Fénix sets up for a Mexican Muscle Buster, but Page rips at his mask. The referee warns Page. Page attacks Fénix from behind and hits an Ego-Plex for the victory.

Winner by Pinfall and still WWE Intercontinental Champion: “All Ego” Ethan Page

Very good match with Ethan Page and Rey Fénix. Fénix is always great, but Page looked particularly good here. The guy is good with the right opponent. I was a little worried that Fénix tweaked his wrist. He was holding it all match, but he seems fine. #WWERaw

Replay: This past Friday on SmackDown , Damian Priest took WWE United States Champion Trick Williams out with a ring bell shot. Williams will not be able to compete at Survivor Series, which removes him from Team USA.

WWE Hall of Famer Rey Mysterio heads to the ring. We’ll hear from the captain of Team Mexico next.

-Commercial Break-

Rey Mysterio addresses who the fifth member of Team Mexico is

WWE Hall of Famer Rey Mysterio is in the ring. Team USA vs. Team Mexico will be unlike anything you’ve seen before. To USA’s credit, their team is stacked. They have LA Knight, Je’Von Evans, CM Punk, and Cody Rhodes as Team Captain. As captain of Team Mexico, Mysterio can say with pride that he has picked the very best that Mexico has to offer. That is himself, Dragon Lee, Rey Fénix, Penta with zero miedo, and… Mysterio says he could say the wait is over and Team Mexico is now complete because he’s going to announce the fifth member of Team Mexico. That is…

“Dirty” Dominik Mysterio heads to the ring with JD McDonagh . Rey shakes his head in anger. McDonagh tells the crowd to shut up and show Dominik some respect. This is a son trying to repair his relationship with his father. Dominik calls Rey, “Dad,” something he hasn’t done in years. Dominik and McDonagh get into the ring. Dominik says he knows his father may have found a fifth member, but he wants to say one thing. Dominik wanted to join Team USA, but after talking to “that bum” LA Knight and “Captain Crybaby,” Dominik realized that the blood that runs through his veins is Mexican. McDonagh shouts, “Viva la raza!” Dominik says he understands they’ve had their differences over the years over the way Rey treated him growing up. Dominik is willing to let bygones be bygones and start over if Rey stops playing around and adds him to Team Mexico. The crowd loudly boos.

Rey looks at his son and says he’s touched by what Dominik said. It sounds like it came from the heart. Rey says he’s building a team of luchadores, not trust fund jackasses. Rey goes to announce the fifth member, but Dominik stops him and says he’s the King of the Luchadores. There is no luchador better than Dominik Mysterio. Rey says there is. If Rey says, “El Grande Americano,” he’ll lose his mind. Dominik says it better not be him. Rey asks if he’ll be quiet so he can announce the fifth member of Team Mexico. Dominik says Americano is a disgrace and not a real Mexican. Rey announces El Grande Americano as the fifth member of Team Mexico.

AAA Mega Champion El Grande Americano makes his entrance to a big ovation. Dominik is infuriated. Americano greets the crowd and says he’s the fifth and final member of Team Mexico. He calls Dominik a “cabron.” In his heart, Americano is much more Mexican than Dominik. To prove that, he spoke with Adam Pearce. Tonight, it’ll be The Judgment Day vs. Rey Mysterio and El Grande Americano right here and now! The crowd loudly cheers.

Tag Team Match 
WWE Hall of Famer Rey Mysterio and AAA Mega Champion El Grande Americano vs. The Judgment Day (“Dirty” Dominik Mysterio and JD McDonagh)

Referee: Danilo Anfibio

McDonagh and Dominik immediately blindside Rey and Americano, stomping away at him. Americano fights back as McDonagh takes Rey down with a drop toehold and drops a leg. McDonagh knees Rey and sends him into the ropes for a tilt-a-whirl backbreaker, but Rey counters out and drops him into position. Dominik pulls McDonagh out of the way of a 619. Americano hits McDonagh with a suicide dive. Rey then follows up with a flying seated senton off the apron onto Dominik.

-Commercial Break-

Back from the break, Americano shoulder tackles McDonagh and sends him into the ropes for a dropkick. Americano hits an arm drag and applies an armbar. McDonagh fights up and hits a jawbreaker. McDonagh chops Americano, but Americano chops him back. Americano hits a textbook vertical suplex for a two-count. Americano whips McDonagh to the opposite corner, but McDonagh twists out of the way and takes him down. McDonagh hits a slingshot corkscrew plancha for a two-count. McDonagh rips at Americano’s mask before tagging Dominik in. The Judgment Day sends Americano into the ropes, but he breaks up a double-team attempt.

Rey tags in and punches away at Dominik. Dominik reverses a whip, but Rey comes back with a head-scissor takeover. Rey gets the crowd going and shoulders Dominik in the corner. Rey climbs the ropes, but Dominik pulls him down. Dominik sends him to the opposite corner and counters a slingshot with an Alabama Slam attempt. Rey slides out and knocks him into position, but Dominik responds with a superkick. Dominik sends Rey into the ropes, but he holds on and boots Dominik back. Rey then clotheslines McDonagh off the apron. Dominik attacks his father from behind and tags McDonagh in. McDonagh hits Rey with a slingshot moonsault, landing on Rey’s face. Dominik tags in and hits a slingshot somersault senton before knocking Americano off the apron. Dominik punches and stomps away at his father for a two-count. Dominik puts Rey on the top rope and hits some crossfaces before pulling him into the tree of woe. Dominik dropkicks Rey in the midsection and covers for a two-count.

-Commercial Break-

We return from the break to see Rey fighting McDonagh away. McDonagh scoops Rey up, but Rey slides off and hits a jawbreaker. McDonagh quickly stops Rey from making a tag. Rey kicks McDonagh away. Dominik tags in and stops his father from making a tag. Dominik starts up the Three Amigos. 1 Amigo… 2 Amigos… and 3 Amigos. McDonagh tags in and drops an elbow before taunting the crowd. Rey starts fighting back, elbows McDonagh, then goes for a springboard moonsault block. McDonagh catches him and tries to slam him, but Rey (sort of) counters with a DDT. McDonagh gets up and lifts him for a vertical suplex, but Rey firmly plants him with a DDT to counter out.

Americano and Dominik tag in. Americano punches Dominik and McDonagh down. Americano hits Dominik with a back body drop, followed by a rolling Death Valley Driver to McDonagh. Americano hits Dominik with an Air Raid Crash for a near fall. Americano gets the crowd going and goes to the top rope, but McDonagh crotches him. Outside the ring, Rey attacks McDonagh, but McDonagh sends him into the ring post. In the ring, Dominik tags McDonagh as he comes off the top rope with a Frog Splash. McDonagh follows up with a moonsault for a near fall.

Dominik tags in and dropkicks Americano into position. Americano avoids a 619, and Rey drops McDonagh into position. Rey hits Dominik and McDonagh with a double 619. Rey then catches McDonagh with a wild hurricanrana over the top rope. Americano then smashes Dominik with an Aztec Headbutt for the win.

Winners by Pinfall: WWE Hall of Famer Rey Mysterio and AAA Mega Champion El Grande Americano

Gorilla Position

Team USA is standing in the Gorilla Position. CM Punk is fired up and wants Cody Rhodes’ music played because he’s the Team Captain.

Team USA addresses who their fifth member will be

Team USA, consisting of “The American Nightmare” Cody Rhodes , CM Punk , World Tag Team Champion Je’Von Evans , and LA Knight , head to the ring as a united front to Rhodes’ theme song. The crowd gives them a big ovation and chants, “USA!” Evans waves the flag in the ring. Rhodes greets the Wichita crowd and asks what they want to talk about. The crowd again chants, “USA.” Rhodes says it’s time to reveal their fifth member.

Je’Von Evans cuts in, saying the crowd isn’t loud enough for him. Evans gets them fired up. Rhodes goes to speak, but Knight interjects and tells him to get to the announcement. Knight then asks Evans if he said, “Yerp.” Knight says there’s a lot of talent in the ring. Even if they had “Hacksaw” Jim Duggan, they’d still win. Knight has love for Mexico, but they’re going to get a star-splattered “kiester-kicking” courtesy of LA Knight. Now, who is the fifth guy?

Rhodes goes to speak, but he stops to ask if anyone has anything else to say. Punk thanks Rhodes because he’s had the weight of the industry on his shoulders. Every feud is personal from a Rogues Gallery of Douchebags he’s assembled. This is the most fun Punk has had since coming back. He knows Rhodes won’t let him down. Rhodes announces that the fifth member of Team USA is…

Seth “Freakin” Rollins .

Punk is not happy and angrily stares at Rhodes. Rhodes looks unhappy. Punk leaves the ring and heads to the back as Rollins gets inside. Rollins smiles.

They definitely have been putting the schmaltz on for this whole Team USA vs. Team Mexico thing, so it was nice to see a little real storyline work here. You knew it was going to be Seth Rollins after the tease on #SmackDown . They did a good job with the reveal. #WWERaw

Sol Ruca vs. Lyra Valkyria

Referee: Jessika Carr

They lock up, and Valkyria takes her down for a one-count. Ruca does a handspring out of a snapmare and takes Valkyria down with a pair of clotheslines. Ruca spins her around and hits a facebuster. Ruca soon knocks her out of the ring and hits a springboard corkscrew plancha to the floor.

-Commercial Break-

Back from the break, Valkyria is stretching out Ruca. Ruca fights up and punches away at Valkyria. Ruca superkicks her down. After a few moments, Ruca lands a strike combo, sending her into the corner. Valkyria blocks a German Suplex and elbows her to the apron. Ruca snaps her off the top rope and hits a springboard frog splash for a two-count. Valkyria fights back with a chop block. Valkyria hits a DDT, followed by a fisherman’s buster for a two-count. Ruca tries to fight back, but Valkyria blocks a German Suplex and hits a spin kick. Valkyria stares at Sky, and Ruca surprises her with a Sol Snatcher for the win.
