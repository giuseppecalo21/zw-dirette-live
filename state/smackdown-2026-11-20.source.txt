WWE SmackDown Results
November 20, 2026
Evansville, Indiana (Ford Center)
Commentary: Michael Cole and Corey Graves
Results by: Roy Nemer of Wrestleview.com

Owens says he needs a match against Sami Zayn for the WWE title and there is only one man that can make that happen, Owens says Nick Aldis but out comes Gunther.

Owens says he’s not Nick Aldis and Gunther says he understands, he says Owens should have stayed on the shelf because he does not belong in a world where Gunther wants to be WWE Champion. Gunther says unlike SummerSlam, he won’t let Owens get in the way again. Owens tells Gunther that Gunther is complaining that Owens was added to the match that Gunther was also added to at SummerSlam and if he is serious.

Gunther says he takes all of this very serious, he takes standing in the ring very serious, he takes being a World champion very serious, he takes ending careers very serious. Gunther says if Owens doesn’t want to end up on the operation table, he should take Gunther’s presence very seriously. He tells Owens that he is not a monster, he will give him a chance that he did not give Finn Balor and that is to get out of his ring and to stay out of his way.

Owens says fine and leaves the ring. Gunther says he has been waiting to get this off his chest for a very long time. Gunther tells Zayn that for his entire WWE career, when he was at his peak, Zayn screwed him when he was Intercontinental Champion at WrestleMania, when he was the special referee in his match against Cody, even when he was his partner at Madison Square Garden. Gunther says Zayn is the only reason why Zayn is not standing as WWE Champion.

Out comes Owens with Nick Aldis and Owens says he left, just like Gunther asked. Owens says he got Aldis, he told Aldis what Gunther did which is threaten him, his career. He was thinking it’s the Holiday season, he is very thankful that he got the chance to get in the ring after 17 months off and Gunther tells him he is the career killer. Owens says he asked if he would slap Gunther in the ring and that would start a fight but he knows Aldis wouldn’t like that. Owens says he spoke to Aldis and Aldis liked his idea. Aldis says he thought Owens against Gunther tonight is a good idea.

Owens says he is thankful that he gets to beat Gunther tonight and happy Thanksgiving. Kevin’s music hits and he leaves.

We cut to an interview from earlier today of Cathy Kelley and Jade Cargill. He says Kelly that she plans on being her, since she set foot in WWE, she has seen Flair. And deep down inside, Jade is everything that Flair wants to be.

-Commercial Break-

Back from commercial, we see The Rock’s announcement on the Pat McAfee Show announcing that he will be at Survivor Series.

WarGames Advantage Match – Jade Cargill vs. Charlotte Flair

The bell rings and we are under way. Jade with right hands but Flair throws Jade into the turnbuckle. Jade with right hands but Flair throws her to the ropes and Flair with a clothesline.

Flair grabs Jade but Jade with right hands. Jade goes for a suplex but Flair lands on her feet. Flair with a kick to the side of the head and Flair with a kick to the face. Flair climbs the second rope but Jade trips her up. Jade with right hands and she goes for the cover but Flair kicks out.

-Commercial Break-

Back from commercial, Flair with chops to the chest. Flair on the ring apron and she hits Jade with a boot to the face. Flair climbs the top rope and lands a cross body. She goes for the cover but Jade kicks out. Flair with an overhead throw. Flair goes for a big boot but Jade moves out of the way. Jade lifts Flair up and slams her onto the mat. She goes for the cover but Flair kicks out.

Jade goes for a kick but Flair moves out of the way and Flair with a spear. Flair climbs the top rope and lands a moon sault. She goes for the cover but Jade kicks out. Flair grabs Jade but Jade kicks her away. Jade with a pump kick. She goes for the cover but Flair kicks out. Jade goes for Jaded but Flair rolls through it and grabs Jade by the leg. Flair with the Figure Four but Jade makes it to the ropes. Jade removes the turnbuckle pad and Flair with a German suplex. Flair with the Natural Selection. She goes for the cover but Jade kicks out. Flair with right hands and Jade with right hands. Jade goes for a chokeslam but Flair lands on her feet and Flair with a big boot. Flair runs towards Jade but Jade trips her and Flair hits the exposed turnbuckle pad. Jade goes for the cover and gets the pin.

Winner: Jade Cargill

We cut backstage and Kevin Owens is stopped by Candice LeRae. She tells him that she knows what he is trying to do is pathetic but Johnny is next. Owens says he will overlook the fact that she slapped him. He says he genuinely likes her but when she sees Zayn that he is going to take that title from him and for her lovely husband, to tell him to stay out of his way. And he needs to get a better haircut.

A War Raiders promo airs where they talk about Bakusai.

-Commercial Break-

Back from commercial, Green says their friendship is done but she did get her Crown Jewel ring back. In comes Blake Monroe, she tells Green that Green went from rags to riches back to rags. Chelsea slaps Monroe and in comes Tiffany Stratton and she hits Green from behind. Stratton grabs the Crown Jewel ring and steals it from her. Blake hits Green and in come WWE officials who break it up.
