AEW Thanksgiving Eve Dynamite Results 
 November 25, 2026 
 Ralston (Omaha), Nebraska (Liberty First Credit Union Arena) 
 Results by: Alex Kahrs of Wrestleview.com

It is Wednesday, my dudes…you know what that means!

Commentary welcomes us to the show before we go to a video looking back at the first week of the Continental Classic. They address Daniel Garcia being pulled from the tournament after sustaining an injury in his match, with Konosuke Takeshita taking his place at a slight disadvantage as he inherits the 0 points earned going into week two. From there, we get some hype for the matches for tonight before heading to ringside for our opening contest!

Out first is Orange Cassidy, followed by “Speedball” Mike Bailey to get this match underway!

Continental Classic Gold League Match: Orange Cassidy (3) vs. ‘Speedball’ Mike Bailey (0)

Code of Honor (handshake) adhered to before the two lock up. Bailey with an arm wringer, but it’s countered by Cassidy…only for Speedball to cartwheel out of it for an arm drag of sorts. He takes Cassidy down, but Orange takes control for a nearfall. They trade offense until Bailey gets Cassidy down for a nearfall, catching Orange by surprise for a backslide that gets a second two count.

Cassidy rolls out of the ring for a breather, returning to lock up with Bailey. The two trade holds once more, leading to an exchange of nearfalls. Orange traps Bailey by the leg, getting a very close two count before Speedball escapes to the outside. Bailey back in the ring, evading an Orange Punch for a kick to the midsection. Cassidy fires back, landing some chops to the chest. Mike goes for a kick but gets caught with a backbreaker by Orange instead, and Cassidy maintains control with a low dropkick.

The fight leaves the ring as Cassidy gets caught with a hard kick, followed by an Asai moonsault by Speedball sending him to the floor. Bailey brings Orange back in the ring for a missile dropkick, and a cover leads to a two count…and a cross arm breaker by Speedball! Cassidy gets to the ropes for a break, leaving the ring as we go to ad break.

-FULL AD BREAK (ON HBO MAX)-

Cassidy in the corner now, sending Bailey’s head into the turnbuckle to regain control. He follows up with some right hands to the head of Speedball, only to take a hard kick as ten minutes remain in the bout. Bailey sends Orange to the apron for a backflip knee drop, but lands on the hardest part of the ring as Cassidy dodges it. He sends Speedball to the floor before going for a dive, but as Speedball is sent to the floor he catches Cassidy with a cross arm breaker!

Bailey gets back for another knee drop but once again it comes up shy, allowing Cassidy to send him into the ring. He goes for an ankle lock, but Speedball kicks his way out of the hold. Orange hits the ropes and lands a DDT for a nearfall. Orange Punch is evaded as Bailey lands a knee strike. Cassidy tries to block a crane kick but gets sent to the mat by Speedball, who ends up taking a Beach Break to the canvas…only to kick out at two on the cover that follows.

Cassidy fires up, trying to take Bailey down for a three count but to no avail. He goes for an Orange Punch, but runs into a spinning roundhouse kick by Speedball…who gets the pin for his first three points!

Winner via pinfall: ‘Speedball’ Mike Bailey (3)

Bailey celebrates as we take a look at the other matches for the tournament happening tonight. From there, we go to a video package looking at Adam Copeland, who is trying to figure himself out in what will be his final year of wrestling.

Backstage, Don Callis talks about the situation with Cope fighting a bear prior to Full Gear, noting that he still couldn’t take down Okada…who emphasizes this with a quick ranking. Callis says that Copeland is officially retired after failing to beat the Rainmaker for the title, and will hold a “retirement ceremony” for him next week. He walks off as Okada lets us know he is thankful…family!

-Commercial Break-

Backstage we hear from the Brawling Birds, who have their eye on the Women’s World Tag Team Championship match later tonight. They promise that the United Empire will be back in full force soon, and Dynamite rolls on as we head back to ringside.

Jon Moxley makes his entrance for our next Continental Classic bout, making his way through the crowd as the crowd cheers him on. We get a promo from Kevin Knight after Collision, with The Jet looking ahead to his bout against Mox after defeating Ciampa. Speaking of The Jet, out comes Kevin Knight as we get this match underway!

Continental Classic Gold League Match: Jon Moxley (0) vs. Kevin Knight (3)
