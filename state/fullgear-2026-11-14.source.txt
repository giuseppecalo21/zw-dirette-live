AEW Full Gear Results 
 November 14, 2026 
 Phoenix, Arizona (Mortgage Matchup Center) 
 Results by: Alex Kahrs of Wrestleview.com

Full Gear Buy In (Panel Discussion and More) – Airing on AEW’s YouTube and social media channels:

Jungle Jack Perry def. “The Beast” Mortos

With that, it’s time to gear up for AEW Full Gear’s main card!

We open with a video package hyping up the main feuds leading into tonight’s show, before heading to ringside for our opening contest. From there we get a video package for this “dream match” as Adam Copeland challenges Kazuchika Okada for the AEW International Championship at Full Gear!

Back at ringside, Cope makes his entrance to a massive pop from the Phoenix crowd, who sings Adam’s theme as he makes his way toward the ring. Out next is the champion as Kazuchika Okada makes his entrance, also getting a good pop from the crowd as he enters the ring to get things underway!

AEW International Championship: Kazuchika Okada (c) vs. Adam Copeland

Big “AEW” chant as the bell rings, with both men staring each other down. The crowd is thrilled for this match, which seems to almost overshadow anything actually happening until the two lock up. Okada takes Cope to the ropes, but the challenger breaks free as the crowd chants “f–k Don Callis.” Exchange of waist locks between both men before they change to another tie up, which ends with Okada getting Adam to the ropes again. Clean break is not so clean as he knees Cope in the gut, but Adam responds with a leg lariat before ducking a Rainmaker lariat…only for Okada to roll out of the way of a spear attempt!

Okada takes a breather on the outside as the ref begins the count, which gets broken as Okada dips into the ring for just a moment before leaving again. Cope goes after the champ, which costs him as he gets sent into the ring steps by the Rainmaker instead. Okada pulls off some of the protective padding from the floor before dipping into the ring to break the ref’s count again, and then goes for a tombstone piledriver…only for the challenger to counter with a body slam to the padded flooring instead.

He sets up a chair to seat Okada upon for a couple shots, including a boot to the face sending the champ to the floor. He picks up Okada, sending him into the barricade multiple times before bringing him back into the ring. Okada quickly manages to roll back out of the ring, but as he climbs onto the apron he gets knocked back down with a knee from the Rated R Superstar…who scares off Callis at ringside, only to take a dropkick into the barricade by the Rainmaker!

Okada once again breaks the ref’s count before attacking the challenger some more, with Callis shouting that Cope’s career ends tonight. Okada gives Don a hug before bringing Cope back into the ring. He mocks Adam with a boot to the head, following up with a neckbreaker to keep the challenger down.

Copeland gets back to his feet, but not for long as Okada takes him back down to the canvas. He watches the challenger try to get back up, smiling after taking him down hard with another DDT. He lands another hard shot on Cope for the cover, but Adam manages to grab the bottom rope to force a break. Cope back on his feet to trade shots, but he takes a boot from the Rainmaker.

Okada hits a scoop slam on the challenger, followed by an elbow drop. He favors his arm but manages to have enough strength to flip the crowd off. Cope stops a Rainmaker lariat before sending the champ down to the mat. He looks for a spear from the corner, but takes a neckbreaker on the knee of Okada instead.

The champ heads out of the ring, grabbing his title to inflict more damage, but the ref stops him before Cope stacks Okada up for a close two count! Both men down as the ref begins a standing count. They manage to get to their feet before charging at each other with a big boot…which ends with a double down of sorts instead, and the ref begins a new count.

The two fight to their feet until Okada catches Cope with a dropkick, but the challenger ducks a Rainmaker to take the champ down…only for Okada to send him to the ropes, getting the opening he needs for a tombstone piledriver! Cope slowly gets to his feet as the champ looks to take him out with his own move…but Adam catches Okada with an uppercut instead!

Copeland back on his feet as he gives Okada a taste of his own medicine to flip him off. He goes to the corner for a spear, but Okada gets out of harm’s way. Callis catches Cope by the ankle, and as the ref admonishes him for this Cope goes for a spear on the champ…running shoulder first into the title instead! Okada follows up with an Impaler DDT, sending the challenger down to the exposed concrete floor.

The ref begins a count as Okada starts to bring Cope to his feet, only to let him stay down as he returns to the ring. The count reaches seven when Okada decides to bring his opponent back into the ring. Okada is smiling as the ref may be looking to call the match off. Instead, Okada goes for the cover…only to pull Cope back up from the mat. He does this again, before bringing Cope to his feet for a Rainmaker lariat to secure the win and retain his title.

Winner via pinfall and still AEW International Champion: Kazuchika Okada

We get a replay of the finish as Okada celebrates his victory outside the ring and up the ramp. The ref checks on Copeland as Okada poses with his title on the stage. The crowd starts to cheer for Cope’s efforts tonight when Okada runs back into the ring to go after him again. Cope breaks away from a Rainmaker attempt as Okada escapes up the ramp, the ref shouting at him that he’s already won the match.

With that we get a video package hyping up our next match, as Davis and Doyle challenge The Young Bucks for the AEW World Tag Team Championship at Full Gear!

Fresh off a dominant Title Eliminator victory to earn this shot, Davis and Doyle make their entrance at ringside. Out next are the champs as The Young Bucks make their way to the ring to get things underway!

AEW World Tag Team Championship: The Young Bucks (c) vs. Davis & Doyle

We start things off with Matt and Doyle, as Davis distracts Matt enough for a beatdown by Doyle. Doyle brings in Nick as the challengers continue to inflict the pain they gave the Bucks weeks ago. Doyle sends Nick over the barricade into the timekeeper’s area while Davis sends Matt back into the ring.

Matt fights off a double team by the pair as Nick makes his way back to the ring. Davis gets a tag as he goes after Matt, but Nick makes a blind tag to bring the champs back into things…until Jake levels them both with a clothesline! Davis sends Nick to the ropes before tagging in Doyle, and the pair lay out Nick before mocking the crowd. Nick sent to the corner, keeping Jake off his game as Matt gets the tag.

Double-team by the Bucks sends Doyle off his feet, before sending Davis to the outside when he tries to come in. He pulls them to the outside, but both challengers are fended off by the Bucks until a dive attempt leads to a double forearm by Davis and Doyle…who send the Bucks crashing into each other!

Matt brought back into the ring as Davis tags in. The challengers pummel him with forearms before dropping him to the canvas, and Davis gets a nearfall as a result. Davis drops Matt again as Doyle sends Nick off the apron, and a tag allows Doyle to get a two count on Matt before tagging Dunkzilla back in. Sitout senton by Davis, who gets a two count before Matt kicks out. Matt fights out of the corner for a roll up, but Davis breaks it up as Doyle then stops him from going for a tag.

Matt goes for a sunset flip, which connects with an assist from Nick for a two count broken up by Doyle. Tag made between Davis and Doyle as they continue to keep Matt away from the corner. Doyle with a bearhug on Matt, going after the previously injured lower back. Matt boxes Doyle’s ears to break free, but is sent to the corner where he knocks Davis off the apron. He goes for a seated senton off the apron but is caught by Davis, who ends up getting taken out by Nick instead!

Back in the ring, Matt fakes out Doyle by calling for a superkick only to drop him with a DDT…and manages to make the tag to Nick! Nick sends Jake to the corner for a flurry of offense before being shoved away by Doyle. Double-team kick in the corner keeps Doyle off his game, before Nick charges at Davis with a dive to the outside. With Davis down, Doyle is caught with a cross body for a two count. Davis ends up crashing onto Doyle when he goes to stop a subsequent pin attempt.

All four men are in the ring now as the Bucks manage to sweep the challengers off their feet. They go up top for a double team, but the slow movement allows Davis and Doyle to stop the champs. Both teams go at it on opposite corners until the Bucks take over with a pair of sunset flips. Senton and elbow drop connect, but the Bucks only get a two count with Doyle officially kicking out.

Matt with a tag as they send the challengers to the outside for a dive, only to be cut off by Davis and Doyle. Matt is brought back into the ring as Doyle lands a powerbomb, and Davis follows up with a Rolling Thunder suplex. Cover by Doyle but it’s only good for two as Nick breaks it up…before sending Davis to the outside and to the floor with a DDT!

Bucks land a series of superkicks on Doyle in the ring, but a BTE Trigger gets barely a two count before Doyle shoves Matt out of the ring to break the pin. They go for another double superkick but get caught with a double powerbomb by Doyle instead. Tag made to Davis…who runs into a double superkick by the champs! Davis fights back to send Matt to the canvas before attacking both Bucks on the ropes…followed by a double piledriver, but that is not quite enough to keep the champs down for three.

Tag made as the challengers look to take out Matt…only for Nick to intercept Davis and send Doyle off the turnbuckle with a German suplex. Bucks with the BTE Trigger on Davis and Doyle, but both men kick out at two to emphasize they’re still in this match!

Matt looks to pick Davis up for a TK Driver, but needs some help with that from Nick…who then gets stopped at the apron by Doyle. Doyle sends Nick crashing into the timekeeper area as Davis drops Matt with a pendulum piledriver…only for Matt to narrowly kick out at two! Doyle with the tag as the challengers go to the corners for a double lariat, but Matt sends Davis out of the ring…and Dunkzilla is kept out by Nick as Matt rolls up Doyle long enough for the win!

Winners via pinfall and still AEW World Tag Team Champions: The Young Bucks

The Bucks are presented with their titles, but don’t get long to celebrate as the Motor City Machine Guns come out to confront…or rather congratulate the champions. They help the Bucks up and shake hands, reminding the champs they are coming after them…when we get a siren throughout the arena. This is followed by some music for a pair of new arrivals in the Kasper Brothers …who turn out to be the men formerly known as the Creeds!

They come down to the ring and immediately go after the Bucks and MCMG…until FTR make their way out for the save! …or maybe not brother, because they join the Guns in beating down the other two teams in the ring. This ends with the Kaspers and FTR shaking hands before leaving the ring.

We go to a video package for our next match, as Hangman Adam Page and Brodido defend the AEW World Trios Championship against Swerve Strickland and The New Level in a Texas Death Match!

Back at ringside, Strickland and New Level make their entrances first as we get a glimpse at the weapons around the ring. We get a video from the “Swerve House” (Silver Dollar) Saloon as Brody King tries to get a Shirley Temple, only to be told to leave. This leads to King getting in a fight with nearly all the men in the saloon, with Hangman Adam Page waiting outside to take out any stragglers. They are joined by Bandido as they ride off into the proverbial sunset on horses to take us back to ringside.

The champs make their way out as commentary talks about the new championship belts themselves, which have small tributes to the late PAC, and with all six men here we get things underway! (A quick note on the rules of this match: simultaneous knockouts OR one man surrendering is the only way to end this match!)

AEW World Trios Championship – Texas Death Match: Hangman Adam Page & Brodido (c) vs. Swerve Strickland & The New Level

All six men go at it in the ring until we’re left with Swerve and Bandido in the ring. Bandido hits a 21 Plex on Swerve before going after the New Level on the outside, while Brody grabs wooden skewers from otuside the ring. The champs set up their opponents on the apron…before driving the skewers into each man’s forehead! All three challengers drop to the floor as the champs celebrate in the ring. They go for a dive to the outside, but run into steel chairs to the face! Kofi and Creed in particular are panicking over the sight of the skewers in their heads as Nana helps remove them.

Creed goes after Brody, setting him up on a chair for a hip attack that drives King into the barricade. He starts mocking Brody, who drops Austin to the floor. We see Creed reach under the ring apron as King goes after him…only to be hit with a trombone wrapped with barbed wire! He plays some tunes before attacking Brody with the instrument again, and then goes to work dealing with the barbed wire on a table before Page goes after him. Meanwhile, Kofi aids Strickland in going after Bandido.

Page grabs some of the cut-off wire and starts to wrap it around his arm when Kofi cuts him off with a kick, bringing him back into the ring. He goes to the ropes but takes a clothesline courtesty of a barbed wire arm from Hangman, who takes out Swerve with it as well.

He starts to wrap the barbed wire around the mouth of Kofi when Creed stops him…and this leads to Creed getting the same treatment, until Swerve slams a chair against Hangman’s back! Bandido comes in with a dropkick, driving that chair into the face of Strickland…but Swerve responds by attacking the ROH World Champion with the chair. Swerve is busted open as he props the chair up on the mat, bringing Bandido to the turnbuckle. The luchador fights back with a kick as Brody brings some more chairs into the ring.

King sets up a bed of chairs as Bandido and Swerve fight it out on the turnbuckle, with the luchador sending Strickland into the chairs with a DDT before New Level get back into the ring. The pair take down Bandido before attacking Brody, who fights back before bringing one of the barbed wire tables into the ring.

He props it against the corner and ends up sending Creed into that corner…only for Austin to dodge a cannonball senton, forcing Brody to get wrapped up in the barbed wire as he leaves the ring with the table broken in half. New Level pull the broken table out before setting that and pieces of the trombone over the body of Brody. They keep Bandido down before Creed gives a shoutout to Cactus Jack with an elbow drop off the apron. We see Hangman back in the ring now, but so too is Swerve Strickland…and each man has a staple gun in his hand as they circle the mat.

They set up a pair of chairs in the center of the ring and have what I can best describe as a “staple off” as they shoot staples into each other’s chests. Bandido comes in to intervene and takes a staple before Swerve gets knocked down by Page. He goes to the apron but is stopped by Nana, before dropping the Prince to the floor..which gives Swerve an opening to take Hangman out!

In comes Brody King, who reveals a glove covered in thumbtacks…that he uses for a chop to the chest of Strickland! Creed comes after him but takes an Iron Claw with the glove by King, who lays him out with a right hand. Brody leaves the ring to grab a bag from under the ring, revealing a pile of even more thumbtacks that is spilled onto the canvas. He goes after Creed, who fights back before landing a fisherman’s buster sending King onto the tacks! Creed fires up as he hits a tope onto Bandido and Page over and over.

This ends with Creed sending Bandido into the ring steps before going back into the ring one more time. Prince Nana has another bag in his hand. Creed pulls something out of the bag…and it’s a Power Glove! Creed really is playing with power, and we’re about to find out how true that is as he hits a Dragon Punch on King…and it explodes in Brody’s face to knock him down to the mat!

With all three champs down the ref begins the count…but Bandido manages to get up to stop it! Page is back up as well as Kofi and Swerve try to deal with them. Creed comes out of the ring to help as Page gets knocked away, bringing Bandido near an oil well to summon fire that gets dangerously close to the luchador’s face! He does it a second time for good measure, but Page fights back with a light tube on Kofi and a Dead Eye on Creed.

He goes back to the ring to go after Swerve and help Brody, but New Level are back on their feet and head to the ring. They prop Page up on some chairs, possibly trying to keep him tied down as they find a window pane to set on top of Hangman. Kofi steps onto the barricade and hits a double leg drop, breaking the glass over Page’s body to send him back down to the floor!

Creed brings another barbed wire table into the ring, but they are distracted by Bandido trying to make the save. Strickland brings out some padlocks on chains…and they level King with one of them before placing a lock in his mouth for a T.I.P. by Kofi! Brody is left laying as the challengers go after Bandido in the ring. Page comes in for the save but is quickly fought off before New Level set up Bandido for a House Call by Swerve…only for King to run into the ring to take the proverbial bullet!

The challengers go for it again, but Bandido starts to fight back. He catches a hurricanrana from Kofi, driving him through the table with a powerbomb instead…but Swerve catches him by surprise, before getting a chain from Nana. He starts to wrap it around the throat of the luchador, who pulls him to the mat instead. He goes to the opposite corner, climbing up top for a frog splash that sends Strickland into the tack pile.

Now it’s Bandido who has the chain, wrapping it around Swerve’s throat before sending him to the apron. Strickland manages to fight back and starts pulling apart Bandido’s mask to big boos from the crowd…until Page runs in for the save! Swerve narrowly avoids getting leveled by a steel chair, and draws blood from the Hangman which he seems to take great delight in as he, uh…lets some of it into his mouth. Ugh.

Page grabs the chair and fights off New Level, but hesitates when he sees Swerve’s bloody grin…only for Strickland to spit Page’s own blood back in his face! Swerve leaves the ring to grab a cinder block, recalling his previous Texas Death Match against the Hangman as he looks for Big Pressure on Page…but Bandido stops him with a kick to the back of the head.

Bandido follows up with an X-Knee to Swerve’s face before moving the cinder block to the middle of the ring. He looks for a 21 Plex to drive Strickland into it, but New Level stop it before dropping him with what looks like the Big Ending onto the edge of the block instead! Brody King comes running in, bringing New Level to the outside before propping barbed wire table pieces over them for a barricade cross body…only to take a Vertebreaker by Strickland onto the ring steps!

Swerve gets back into the ring, but Page is there as well as he takes out the challenger with a Buckshot Lariat…but a second one is stopped with a chair shot by Strickland! All six men are down as the ref begins the count. It gets to eight as Swerve manages to get to his feet, and right after the count of nine Page is only up enough to take a House Call that knocks him out for the final count! We have new trios champions!

Winners via knockout and new AEW World Trios Champions: Swerve Strickland & The New Level

We get a replay of the big moments of this match as the new champions celebrate up the ramp and on the stage. We go backstage to see Don Callis talking to Kyle Fletcher and Ospreay gets a quick sparring session with Speedball ahead of defending the World Championship.

Commentary talks about Perry taking out Mortos with a muscle buster during the Tailgate Brawl, and the fact Samoa Joe could not make it to the show due to travel issues…but we do get a message FROM Samoa Joe, who has managed to find out what Perry did earlier. He addresses beating Jack in France and how that wasn’t enough for Perry to back away. Jack won’t worry about shaking Joe’s hand, when he is left shaking because of the Coquina Clutch in the middle of the ring.

We get a video package hyping up the AEW Women’s World Tag Team Championship match up next, as Wrathlander challenge the Brawling Birds!

Back at ringside, we get a video of Wrathlander discussing their first PPV entrance as a team. They come up with ideas before they’re interrupted by Billy Gunn, who they tell off before Tony Khan appears to tell them the video package is over and it’s time to go. Wrathlander tell each other “let’s f–king go” before we head back to ringside for the arrival of the challengers to a big pop from the crowd. The Brawling Birds also get a good reception on their way to the ring, before we get things underway!

AEW Women’s World Tag Team Championship: The Brawling Birds (c) vs. Wrathlander

We start off with Statlander and Windsor as they fight to the corner. They break free before trading shots, with Windsor blocking a Stat shoulder tackle before landing an elbow strike. She hits the ring but is dropped as Kris connects with a shoulder tackle this time. Windsor fights back from her feet for a tag to Hayter and drops Cameron from the apron as Jamie rolls Kris up for two.

Stat starts to fight back before making a tag to Harley for a double-team, which gets Cameron a nearfall. Tag to Windsor as Harley does the splits to avoid a clothesline. Blind tag by Stat as Harley takes Alex to the mat with a headscissors. She keeps Windsor down for a kick by Kris, who takes her to the floor outside while Harley brings down Hayter. Jamie fights back with a suplex on Cameron before taking out Stat once more, while Windsor drops Cameron back to the floor.

Ref’s count is broken when Windsor brings Statlander back in the ring for a nearfall. Tag made to Hayer as the champs double-team Stat, but there is a slight mixup when Windsor starts to go for the cover. That allows Stat to kick out at two when Hayter makes the cover instead. Tag made to Cameron, who dodges the attack of Windsor for a thrust kick.

Brawling Birds fight back with a double-team allowing Jamie to get a two count. Harley starts to fight back before Hayter knocks Stat off the apron, and a tag is made to Windsor who fires off some shots on Cameron for a nearfall. Harley tries to fight back, eventually catching Windsor with a back suplex before tagging in Statlander. Hayter tags in as well and they trade offense, with Stat landing a Michinoku Driver for two.

Things break down as all four women go at it, with Harley landing some hard kicks on Hayter only to get laid out by Windsor…who gets leveled with a clothesline by Statlander! Hate Breaker on Cameron and the Birds have both challengers down as Jamie gets a two count.

Hayter and Harley go at it off a tag, until Stat gives an assist for a crucifix pin by Harley. Windsor breaks it up just in time and all four women are down. Stat is back up, but gets sent to the outside by the champs as they go after Cameron…but Kris is back in, and gets Windsor on her shoulders. This leads to a chicken fight between Cameron and Windsor until the latter drops Statlander with a reverse rana.

Harley and Jamie are left to trade shots on each other in the ring, with Harley landing a roundhouse kick. Hayter fires back with one of her own, but a Hayterade is blocked with a drop toe hold and a half crab…until Windsor breaks it up! A pair of lariats from Hayter and she goes for the cover…but Kris breaks it up!

Stat with the tag now but she gets set up for Two Birds One Stone…only for Harley to stop Hayter, pulling her out of the ring as Stat drops Windsor to the canvas for the pin and the win!

Winners via pinfall and new AEW Women’s World Tag Team Champions: Wrathlander

The now-former champs eventually shake hands with the better women on this night, and Wrathlander leave the ring to celebrate up the ramp. The win makes Kris Statlander a triple crown winner and gives Harley Cameron the distinction of being the first two-time Women’s World Tag Team Champion.

We go to the video promoting AEW Thanksgiving Eve Dynamite and Thanksgiving Collision from Omaha, Nebraska before going to ringside for our next match, as a video package hypes us up for Tommaso Ciampa defending the AEW National Championship against Ilja Dragunov at Full Gear!

Back at ringside, Dragunov makes his entrance first before the champion makes his way to the ring to get this match underway!

AEW National Championship: Tommaso Ciampa (c) vs. Ilja Dragunov

Chants from the crowd as the two men circle the mat before locking up. Ciampa is taken to the mat by Dragunov, but the champ (uh) breaks free to get to his feet. They go at it again, which ends with an armbar of sorts from Ilja. Ciampa finds a way out, and gets his foot on the rope to break a pin attempt.

Tommaso gets the challenger to the corner and is forced to make a break, which gives Dragunov an opening to strike back. This leads to the two trading shots back and forth, until Ciampa catches Ilja with a side headlock. Dragunov counters to take the champ down for some right hands, but Ciampa turns it around for some shots of his own. Both men back up but Dragunov takes Ciampa down hard for a nearfall.

The two grapple until Dragunov sends Ciampa flying with a modified Magistral, and then sends him to the outside with a knee strike. Ciampa reaches for the hood of the announce table to stop a German suplex by Dragunov, and the champ runs into the ring to build up speed for a knee strike sending Dragunov off the apron to the floor! Tommaso lets us know that Ciampa Rules before going after the challenger, sending him into the barricade and driving his boot into Ilja’s face.

Dragunov starts to fight back, propping the champ up against the table for some elbows…only to be sent face first onto it by Tommaso, who then slams him against the ring steps! He mocks the challenger by sitting on the apron, giving himself applause and a pat on the back before going after the arm of Dragunov. He brings Ilja back into the ring for some more damage, stomping away at the arm for good measure.

Top wristlock into an elbow driver on the challenger, and Ciampa follows up with some kicks to the ribs. He goes back after the arm, but Dragunov breaks free before sending the champ to the ropes for a chop. This hurts the already injured arm and Ilja drops to the mat, allowing Tommaso to go back to work on him.

Ciampa then blocks a German suplex and dodges the challenger from the ropes, landing a knee strike. He goes for a second one with the kneepad down, but Dragunov clips him with his injured hand! Ilja tries to roll up the champ twice, each one getting a nearfall. He goes after the knee of Ciampa, who blocks a lariat only to take an enziguri by the challenger! He follows up with some headbutts and forearms followed by a senton.

Ciampa starts to fight back, but takes a German suplex by Dragunov for two. Ciampa stops him from going for another one, but gets laid out with a clothesline instead. Ciampa takes a D-Bomb to the leg as Ilja pulls him to the corner, and it looks like Ciampa is struggling as he gets set up on the top turnbuckle by Dragunov.

Tommaso bites the challenger’s hand to fight him off before dropping him to the canvas with an avalanche Air Raid Crash…but it looks like it caused some more damage to his leg as well, as he struggles to capitalize. Both men eventually on their feet as they trade shots, only for Ciampa to send Ilja out of the ring. He attacks the challenger on the floor before sending him face first onto the ring steps…and then picks him up for an Air Raid Crash on the edge of the steps! He brings Dragunov back in the ring for a running knee and the cover…but the challenger kicks out at two!

The fight makes its way to the apron as they fight it out some more, with Dragunov winning the fight as he takes the champ onto the hardest part of the ring with a suplex…and follows with a D-Bomb off the apron onto Ciampa’s head on the floor! The ref begins the count as the challenger struggles to get Tommaso to his feet, and both men are back in the ring around eight.

Dragunov takes the champ to the top turnbuckle for some headbutts, followed by a superplex sending Ciampa crashing against the canvas. He goes for a D-Bomb, but Ciampa dodges it and connects with a Fujiwara armbar. Ciampa then transitions into a running knee, with Dragunov dodging a second one before taking it the third time around…but it’s only good for two as the challenger kicks out!

Tommaso quickly goes for another armbar, but Dragunov breaks free before connecting with not one but two more D-Bombs to pick up the win! That’s three title changes in a row, folks!

Winner via pinfall and new AEW National Champion: Ilja Dragunov

Ilja celebrates with the title as we see Tommaso Ciampa trying to figure out his next steps after losing the title so quickly. With that, we go to a video package hyping up the start of the Continental Classic this Wednesday on Dynamite, and then another package hyping up our next match as Marina Shafir challenge Mercedes Moné for the AEW Women’s World Championship!

Back at ringside, Marina Shafir makes her entrance through the crowd to a big reaction from the Phoenix crowd…who give a similar reaction for the arrival of the champion as Mercedes Moné makes her way to the ring. We get introductions for the challenger and the champion (who noticeably gets more boos by this poitn) as we get this match underway!

AEW Women’s World Championship: Mercedes Moné (c) vs. Marina Shafir

Moné tries to avoid Shafir at first before going for a waistlock, which gets countered into a takedown by Marina. She ties up the champion for a unique submission, largely just to show that she can do it before she breaks the hold to flex on Moné. Mercedes doesn’t take kindly to this as she goes on the attack, sending the challenger to the corner. Marina blocks a monkey flip and manages to stop a Statement Maker that comes after. A series of throws by the challenger, with Mercedes blocking the last of these before going for a Money Maker. Marina blocks it and twists the arm of Moné to fully break free.

Mercedes looks for a rear naked choke on Shafir, who sends the champ to the corner before breaking free…only for Moné to knock her down for a double stomp to the back! Moné goes to work on Marina in the corner now, taking her down to the mat before mocking Shafir. She puts Marina in a hammerlock before transitioning into an armbreaker shades of either Swerve Strickland or Penta, but a resulting pin is stopped at one as Marina kicks out. Mercedes back at it as she gets a nearfall before going for an armbar.

The grip is not fully in place before Marina breaks free, spinning the champ around with a throw to the mat. Shafir fires off some more shots including a spinal tap before going for the cover, but Moné kicks out at two. Moné goes after the challenger, but Marina twists her around for a nearfall as we get dueling chants of “She’s our Problem” and “CEO”…and Moné connects with a Meteora for a two count!

Mercedes shouts down at her challenger before landing a slap to the face…with Shafir firing back with a callback to her Death Rider pal Daniel Garcia with a dance to mock the champ! Moné goes for a headscissors into an armbar, but Marina keeps the grip from being in tight before flipping off the champ…and then stomping her bare foot into the face of Moné!
