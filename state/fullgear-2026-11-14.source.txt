With that, it’s time to gear up for AEW Full Gear’s main card!

We open with a video package hyping up the main feuds leading into tonight’s show, before heading to ringside for our opening contest. From there we get a video package for this “dream match” as Adam Copeland challenges Kazuchika Okada for the AEW International Championship at Full Gear!

Back at ringside, Cope makes his entrance to a massive pop from the Phoenix crowd, who sings Adam’s theme as he makes his way toward the ring. Out next is the champion as Kazuchika Okada makes his entrance, also getting a good pop from the crowd as he enters the ring to get things underway!

AEW International Championship: Kazuchika Okada (c) vs. Adam Copeland

Big “AEW” chant as the bell rings, with both men staring each other down. The crowd is thrilled for this match, which seems to almost overshadow anything actually happening until the two lock up. Okada takes Cope to the ropes, but the challenger breaks free as the crowd chants “f–k Don Callis.” Exchange of waist locks between both men before they change to another tie up, which ends with Okada getting Adam to the ropes again. Clean break is not so clean as he knees Cope in the gut, but Adam responds with a leg lariat before ducking a Rainmaker lariat…only for Okada to roll out of the way of a spear attempt!

Okada takes a breather on the outside as the ref begins the count, which gets broken as Okada dips into the ring for just a moment before leaving again. Cope goes after the champ, which costs him as he gets sent into the ring steps by the Rainmaker instead. Okada pulls off some of the protective padding from the floor before dipping into the ring to break the ref’s count again, and then goes for a tombstone piledriver…only for the challenger to counter with a body slam to the padded flooring instead.

He sets up a chair to seat Okada upon for a couple shots, including a boot to the face sending the champ to the floor. He picks up Okada, sending him into the barricade multiple times before bringing him back into the ring. Okada quickly manages to roll back out of the ring, but as he climbs onto the apron he gets knocked back down with a knee from the Rated R Superstar…who scares off Callis at ringside, only to take a dropkick into the barricade by the Rainmaker!

Okada once again breaks the ref’s count before attacking the challenger some more, with Callis shouting that Cope’s career ends tonight. Okada gives Don a hug before bringing Cope back into the ring. He mocks Adam with a boot to the head, following up with a neckbreaker to keep the challenger down.

Copeland gets back to his feet, but not for long as Okada takes him back down to the canvas. He watches the challenger try to get back up, smiling after taking him down hard with another DDT. He lands another hard shot on Cope for the cover, but Adam manages to grab the bottom rope to force a break. Cope back on his feet to trade shots, but he takes a boot from the Rainmaker.

Okada hits a scoop slam on the challenger, followed by an elbow drop. He favors his arm but manages to have enough strength to flip the crowd off. Cope stops a Rainmaker lariat before sending the champ down to the mat. He looks for a spear from the corner, but takes a neckbreaker on the knee of Okada instead.

The champ heads out of the ring, grabbing his title to inflict more damage, but the ref stops him before Cope stacks Okada up for a close two count! Both men down as the ref begins a standing count. They manage to get to their feet before charging at each other with a big boot…which ends with a double down of sorts instead, and the ref begins a new count.

The two fight to their feet until Okada catches Cope with a dropkick, but the challenger ducks a Rainmaker to take the champ down…only for Okada to send him to the ropes, getting the opening he needs for a tombstone piledriver! Cope slowly gets to his feet as the champ looks to take him out with his own move…but Adam catches Okada with an uppercut instead!

Copeland back on his feet as he gives Okada a taste of his own medicine to flip him off. He goes to the corner for a spear, but Okada gets out of harm’s way. Callis catches Cope by the ankle, and as the ref admonishes him for this Cope goes for a spear on the champ…running shoulder first into the title instead! Okada follows up with an Impaler DDT, sending the challenger down to the exposed concrete floor.

The ref begins a count as Okada starts to bring Cope to his feet, only to let him stay down as he returns to the ring. The count reaches seven when Okada decides to bring his opponent back into the ring. Okada is smiling as the ref may be looking to call the match off. Instead, Okada goes for the cover…only to pull Cope back up from the mat. He does this again, before bringing Cope to his feet for a Rainmaker lariat to secure the win and retain his title.

Winner via pinfall and still AEW International Champion: Kazuchika Okada

We get a replay of the finish as Okada celebrates his victory outside the ring and up the ramp. The ref checks on Copeland as Okada poses with his title on the stage. The crowd starts to cheer for Cope’s efforts tonight when Okada runs back into the ring to go after him again. Cope breaks away from a Rainmaker attempt as Okada escapes up the ramp, the ref shouting at him that he’s already won the match.

With that we get a video package hyping up our next match, as Davis and Doyle challenge The Young Bucks for the AEW World Tag Team Championship at Full Gear!

Fresh off a dominant Title Eliminator victory to earn this shot, Davis and Doyle make their entrance at ringside. Out next are the champs as The Young Bucks make their way to the ring to get things underway!

AEW World Tag Team Championship: The Young Bucks (c) vs. Davis & Doyle

We start things off with Matt and Doyle, as Davis distracts Matt enough for a beatdown by Doyle. Doyle brings in Nick as the challengers continue to inflict the pain they gave the Bucks weeks ago. Doyle sends Nick over the barricade into the timekeeper’s area while Davis sends Matt back into the ring.

Matt fights off a double team by the pair as Nick makes his way back to the ring. Davis gets a tag as he goes after Matt, but Nick makes a blind tag to bring the champs back into things…until Jake levels them both with a clothesline! Davis sends Nick to the ropes before tagging in Doyle, and the pair lay out Nick before mocking the crowd. Nick sent to the corner, keeping Jake off his game as Matt gets the tag.

Double-team by the Bucks sends Doyle off his feet, before sending Davis to the outside when he tries to come in. He pulls them to the outside, but both challengers are fended off by the Bucks until a dive attempt leads to a double forearm by Davis and Doyle…who send the Bucks crashing into each other!

Matt brought back into the ring as Davis tags in. The challengers pummel him with forearms before dropping him to the canvas, and Davis gets a nearfall as a result. Davis drops Matt again as Doyle sends Nick off the apron, and a tag allows Doyle to get a two count on Matt before tagging Dunkzilla back in. Sitout senton by Davis, who gets a two count before Matt kicks out. Matt fights out of the corner for a roll up, but Davis breaks it up as Doyle then stops him from going for a tag.

Matt goes for a sunset flip, which connects with an assist from Nick for a two count broken up by Doyle. Tag made between Davis and Doyle as they continue to keep Matt away from the corner. Doyle with a bearhug on Matt, going after the previously injured lower back. Matt boxes Doyle’s ears to break free, but is sent to the corner where he knocks Davis off the apron. He goes for a seated senton off the apron but is caught by Davis, who ends up getting taken out by Nick instead!

Back in the ring, Matt fakes out Doyle by calling for a superkick only to drop him with a DDT…and manages to make the tag to Nick! Nick sends Jake to the corner for a flurry of offense before being shoved away by Doyle. Double-team kick in the corner keeps Doyle off his game, before Nick charges at Davis with a dive to the outside. With Davis down, Doyle is caught with a cross body for a two count. Davis ends up crashing onto Doyle when he goes to stop a subsequent pin attempt.

All four men are in the ring now as the Bucks manage to sweep the challengers off their feet. They go up top for a double team, but the slow movement allows Davis and Doyle to stop the champs. Both teams go at it on opposite corners until the Bucks take over with a pair of sunset flips. Senton and elbow drop connect, but the Bucks only get a two count with Doyle officially kicking out.

Matt with a tag as they send the challengers to the outside for a dive, only to be cut off by Davis and Doyle. Matt is brought back into the ring as Doyle lands a powerbomb, and Davis follows up with a Rolling Thunder suplex. Cover by Doyle but it’s only good for two as Nick breaks it up…before sending Davis to the outside and to the floor with a DDT!

Bucks land a series of superkicks on Doyle in the ring, but a BTE Trigger gets barely a two count before Doyle shoves Matt out of the ring to break the pin. They go for another double superkick but get caught with a double powerbomb by Doyle instead. Tag made to Davis…who runs into a double superkick by the champs! Davis fights back to send Matt to the canvas before attacking both Bucks on the ropes…followed by a double piledriver, but that is not quite enough to keep the champs down for three.

Tag made as the challengers look to take out Matt…only for Nick to intercept Davis and send Doyle off the turnbuckle with a German suplex. Bucks with the BTE Trigger on Davis and Doyle, but both men kick out at two to emphasize they’re still in this match!

Matt looks to pick Davis up for a TK Driver, but needs some help with that from Nick…who then gets stopped at the apron by Doyle. Doyle sends Nick crashing into the timekeeper area as Davis drops Matt with a pendulum piledriver…only for Matt to narrowly kick out at two! Doyle with the tag as the challengers go to the corners for a double lariat, but Matt sends Davis out of the ring…and Dunkzilla is kept out by Nick as Matt rolls up Doyle long enough for the win!

Winners via pinfall and still AEW World Tag Team Champions: The Young Bucks
