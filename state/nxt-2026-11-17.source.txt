The show opens with commentary welcoming us to the show.

Tony D’Angelo makes his entrance ahead of our opening contest, followed shortly thereafter by Kam Hendrix to get us going.

Kam Hendrix vs. Tony D’Angelo

D’Angelo hot out the gates, big right hooks, sending Hendrix to the apron and hip tossing him back inside, to the corner, smashing his face into the turnbuckles corner to corner! Kam manages to turn things around, gets some clubbing blows in, hooks Tony for a suplex but he slips out and bowls him over with a shoulder block!

Back and forth, big chop from Hendrix, laying ‘em in, D’Angelo whips him across and lands a vicious elbow, cover for two! Hard whip, Kam comes up lame and limping but it’s a ruse to let him get a sucker punch off! Strutting, Tony nails him out of nowhere and sends him crashing to the floor! Hendrix frustrated, stomping around ringside, he takes some candy off of Vic’s side but doesn’t like it and spits it out as we go to break!

Back from commercial, Kam takes control with the Showtime kick, back body drop, cover for two! Pressing the attack, Cobra Clutch applied, dragging D’Angelo back to his feet, clubbing him down, whip to the corner, back body drop puts Hendrix on the apron! He goes up top, Tony cuts him off and hits the superplex!

D’Angelo throwing suplexes, one-handed bulldog, pop-up powerslam… NOPE! Back and forth, Tony hits the spear… NO GOOD! Kam goes to the floor and clears the announce desk and grabs a chair and starts swinging but D’Angelo ducks the chair and drops him face-first into the announce desk! Tony wants a chair but Myles Borne is there to stand on it and Hendrix slams D’Angelo onto the chair he had been swinging moments earlier!

Back inside…

Kam Hendrix wins by pinfall with Lights, Kam, Action.

She says it’s not a Tuesday if Zaria isn’t out here lying to everyone, is it? She’s so angry, using such vulgar language, why oh why could that possibly be? It’s not because John Cena picked her to be in the John Cena Classic, is it? It’s pretty obvious why he did though, she’s a good person, not a liar like Zaria.

Zaria isn’t emotionally stable enough to look at her, let alone carry the title, so it’s time for Dame to take the one thing she has left to hide behind and become Izzi Two Times.

Zaria says it’s laughable that Izzi thinks she’s a good person when she’s a parasite hiding behind a halo she built for herself, and Cena can hand her a spot in the Classic, but the title isn’t a handout and she doesn’t think Dame is strong enough to rip it out of her hands. She’s done with the psychological manipulation, and when the bell rings she’s gonna make Izzi regret all of that.

She gets her title shot, but Zaria gets something better, the ability to prove she is exactly who she says she is, and she’ll leave unbothered, unstoppable, and unshakable as North American Champion, and she punctuates that by slipping in and hitting the F-5!
