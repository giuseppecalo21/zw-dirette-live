Fort Lauderdale, Florida (Amerant Bank Arena)
Commentary: Michael Cole and Corey Graves
Results by: Mike Tedesco of Wrestleview.com

“Then. Now. Forever. Together.” The WWE video leads us into the show.

Video: This past Saturday at WWE Crown Jewel , Cody Rhodes punted Randy Orton in the head to win a Last Man Standing Match. World Heavyweight Champion Roman Reigns also showed respect to Undisputed WWE Champion Sami Zayn. Finally, Tiffany Stratton cashed in Money in the Bank on Chelsea Green, winning the WWE Women’s Championship.

John Cena reveals the next invitee to the John Cena Classic

John Cena comes out to a MASSIVE reception. Cena tells Stew they have to hit their time, then runs down to the ring. The John Cena Classic Championship is in the ring, and Cena spins the spinner belt. The crowd is going crazy for Cena. Cena says they don’t even need a warm-up. Cena holds up the spinner belt and says, “The Championship is here!”

On December 14, in a history-making event, live just a little up the road in Orlando, Florida, we will find out what happens to this title. The John Cena Classic is an evening of opportunity. On that night, a record-setting number of NXT Superstars will have their Raw debut matches against the best and the brightest that WWE has to offer, with all superstars having the opportunity to become a champ. The biggest opportunity of the night goes to their biggest superstar, the fans. They can use their voice to make sure it’s heard loud and clear.

That’s December 14. How about we talk about right here and now? The time is now. It’s time to see who’s next. The next invitee to the John Cena Classic is… Charlotte Flair . The 14-time Women’s Champion heads to the ring. She gets on the apron and waits for an inexperienced stagehand to hold the ropes open for her. Flair finally gets in the ring and embraces Cena.

Flair says she’s her father’s daughter and tends to get emotional like him. She hopes she can make it through this. This is for Cena. For 10 years, she’s been trying to figure out how to get into a storyline to get in the ring and stand across from Cena. Flair has been trying to be a part of that magic. She’s dreamt it and manifested it to be in the ring with Cena because she thinks she’s that great, too. She knows her dad is at home in Tampa bawling with every TV on at that bar. Cena waves to Ric Flair. The reason why she’s here is that Cena wanted her to be part of his legacy. That means so much to her.

Flair doesn’t want to cry because her makeup is fabulous. Two years ago, after WrestleMania 41, she was backstage. Cena walked past her. She wasn’t wrestling. She was depressed and didn’t know if she wanted to be part of the company anymore or keep wrestling. Flair felt so lost. Cena looked her straight in the eyes and asked what happened in Chicago. Flair said she didn’t have an answer and “shit the bed.” It wasn’t her night. Cena looked at her and told her to stop trying to be perfect. Cena said, “Own who you are.” Since then, for the last two years, she’s gotten high-fives and been cheered. Flair isn’t saying she’s a fan favorite. She thanks Cena for helping her put on her big-girl pants, find her passion, and reinvent herself. Cena didn’t do it because she’s Ric Flair’s kid. Cena believed in her when she didn’t. Flair starts to cry. The crowd chants, “You deserve it.” Flair says when she doesn’t believe in herself, she’ll remember being in the John Cena Classic, being excellent in the John Cena Classic, and winning the John Cena Championship. Flair says she’s the greatest champion of all time. WOO!

Cena says he’s going to go over his time, but he doesn’t care. When she started in the business, people thought she had an easy way in. Flair told him about the weight of carrying her father’s legacy, her brother, as well as rebuilding women’s wrestling and giving opportunities to today’s superstars. She never had it easy. She just made it look easy. There was a time when he said he needed to win 17 because he wanted to shake hands with whoever would win 18. Cena was always talking about her. Cena thanks her and embraces her before raising her arm.

Locker Room Segment

SmackDown General Manager Nick Aldis greets WWE Hall of Famer Rey Mysterio . Mysterio has an idea of someone who he could add to Team Mexico. Cody Rhodes walks in, greets Mysterio and Aldis. Mysterio says they have business, so he walks off. Aldis asks Rhodes about Crown Jewel and whether he’s in a good place, ready to leave that business behind. Rhodes says Crown Jewel will stick with him. Maybe he uses the Punt. Maybe not. Still, his attention is on Survivor Series. Aldis asks how recruiting is going for Team USA. Rhodes has some people in mind, as does CM Punk. Maybe they’ll add new members tonight or on Raw. After Survivor Series is behind them, Rhodes wants to revisit the No. 1 Contender conversation.

WWE Tag Team Champions MFT make their entrance, accompanied by WWE Hall of Famer Haku. They’ll defend the titles against Bakusai next.

-Commercial Break-

WWE Tag Team Championship 
MFT (Tama Tonga and Talla Tonga) w/ Haku vs. Bakusai (Shinsuke Nakamura and Kyoki)

Referee: Jason Ayers

The bell rings, and Kyoki hits Tama Tonga with a pair of running crossbody blocks. Tama avoids one, but Kyoki comes right back with one. Tama quickly takes Kyoki down and tags Talla Tonga. Talla whips Kyoki hard into the corner before chopping him down. Talla stands him up and chops him down again. Tama tags in and chops Kyoki, but Kyoki begs for more. Kyoki hits the ropes and avoids a clothesline. Tama tries to block a running hurricanrana, but Kyoki takes him down. Kyoki attacks Talla, but he doesn’t knock him off the apron. As Talla tries to get into the ring, Kyoki kicks the rope into his groin. Tama puts Kyoki on the apron, but Kyoki knocks him back before kicking Talla in the face. Haku then breaks his Tongan Walking Stick on Kyoki’s knee.

-Commercial Break-

Back from the break, Kyoki fights up from a rear chin lock applied by Tama. Tama sends Kyoki into the corner, but he runs into a boot. Kyoki blocks a kick, but Tama punches him. Kyoki soon fights back with a dragon screw.

Shinsuke Nakamura tags in and punches Tama before kicking him in the chest. Nakamura gives Tama some Good Vibrations. Tama reverses a whip to the opposite corner, but Nakamura sidesteps him. Nakamura kicks him in the neck before laying him across the top rope for a knee to the ribs. Nakamura knocks Talla off the apron and sizes Tama up for a Kinshasa. Nakamura catches him with a rebound wheel kick and goes to the corner. Tama avoids a Kinshasa and hits a Cutthroat Running Elbow. Tama goes for a double underhook move, but Nakamura counters with a back body drop before kneeing him in the head. Kyoki attacks Talla before he gets into the ring. Nakamura and Kyoki then give Talla a double clothesline over the top rope. Haku checks on Talla, and Kyoki baseball slides Haku into Talla! Kyoki then hits Talla with a missile dropkick off the apron!

In the ring, Nakamura hits Tama with a Kinshasa! 1… 2… The War Raiders run in and attack Nakamura.

Winners by Disqualification: Bakusai

The War Raiders destroy everyone in the ring, finishing with a Ragnaroc on Nakamura.
