AEW Dynamite Results 
 November 18, 2026 
 Denver, Colorado (Mission Ballroom) 
 Results by: Alex Kahrs of Wrestleview.com

It is Wednesday, my dudes…you know what that means!

We open with a video package featuring highlights from a wild and crazy Full Gear pay-per-view, including three title changes in a row and culminating in Will Ospreay retaining the AEW World Championship against Kyle Fletcher. Speaking of, Lexy Nair tried to get some words from Ospreay after the show, but he said he’d talk to her at Dynamite as he approaches Kyle Fletcher in the trainer’s room.

Callis shuffles off as Ospreay talks to his former friend, admitting that he’s proud of him. Fletcher isn’t having any of it, saying he’s getting tired of being told how much potential he has when he keeps failing at the most important times. He feels like something is not working, like something is wrong. Ospreay says he’s been there and Kyle knows it, as someone who was in the room when he fell.

He tells Kyle to take or leave the advice he’s about to give him, noting that the person he is on TikTok and his vlogs and elsewhere is different from when he’s around Don Callis. He tells Fletcher to get away from Callis, and THEN he will find Kyle Fletcher. Kyle asks who that even is, and who he’s supposed to be…but Will says that’s up for Kyle to decide, not Don Callis. For what it’s worth, Ospreay forgives Kyle…and there will always be a spot in the United Empire for him.

Kyle gets up from the bench, walking away as we head back to ringside. What we just saw took place before Ospreay was evaluated by their medical team, and it was determined that he was not cleared to travel so he will not be here tonight. Commentary pivots from this to focus on the Continental Classic, going over the rules (20 minute time limit, no one at ringside, etc) and the matches set for tonight.

With that, we get ready for the first of these matches as our opening contest! Making his way out first is Orange Cassidy, rocking the “Jane” theme as he walks down the ramp. Out next is the champ, whose title is “held up” during the duration of this tournament as he makes his way through the crowd to ringside. Mox enters the ring as this match gets underway!

Continental Classic Tournament Gold League Match: Jon Moxley (0) vs. Orange Cassidy (0)

After a slow start the two men lock up, exchanging arm wringers until Mox takes Cassidy to the mat. He cinches in a hammerlock as Orange gets to his feet, getting one on the ropes for a break. Side headlock takeover by Cassidy, who transitions into a crucifix pin before Mox counters for a nearfall. Both men back on their feet, only for the champ to take Cassidy back down as he looks for an STF. Quick transition to a straitjacket hold, but Orange gets back up and forces him to the ropes for a break. Clean break as Cassidy backs off, slowly “shoving” the champ. He starts to put his hands in his pockets (as he is want to do), getting Mox a little riled up…only for the champ to get rocked with an Orange Punch instead!

The two leave the ring, where Cassidy sends Mox to the barricade for some mounted punches before bringing the champ back in. He goes for an Orange Punch but gets dropped to the mat, only to fight back with a Stundog Millionaire. Orange up top for a cross body, but Mox rolls through for a cross arm breaker. Cassidy gets up enough to get Mox on his shoulders for a nearfall and the break, but gets leveled with a lariat by the champ as we go to an ad break.

-FULL AD BREAK (ON HBO MAX)-

We come back just as Cassidy tries to counter out of a piledriver for a Beach Break…but to no avail, as Mox connects with the Gotch style piledriver! Cassidy slowly gets to his feet to recover, fending off the champ at the turnbuckle. Mox rakes the back of Orange deep, only to be shoved away by Cassidy…who connects with a missile dropkick taking the champ to the canvas.

Mox blocks a punch from Orange for a sleeper, but Cassidy fights back with a kick to the knee followed by a dragon screw leg whip as ten minutes remain. Mox escapes the ring, but Cassidy stays on top of him as he continues to attack the leg before bringing the champ back in…only to be dropped with a Paradigm Shift! Mox gets a nearfall before transitioning to a bulldog choke, but Orange gets to his feet and reaches the ropes for a break. Mox lands some body shots on him, but Cassidy catches him with an arm drag before cinching in an ankle lock! The champ is in dangerously familiar territory as he crawls toward the ropes…but Cassidy pulls him to the center of the ring!

Grapevine applied as Mox crawls toward the ropes again. Orange breaks the hold just enough to reposition, but gets cradled for two by the champ. Cassidy fires back with a PK and takes the champ down hard with a Beach Break for a close two count. Cassidy gets to his feet in the corner, only to be rocked with a pair of lariats by Mox. He starts to fight back before Mox takes a bite out of him to keep control. Cassidy stops a DDT attempt for a headbutt, positioning the champ for an avalanche Beach Break! Cover by Orange, but Mox narrowly gets a shoulder up before the three. Mox cinches in a bulldog choke as five minutes remain.

Cassidy fights the champ off, looking for an ankle lock before going for an Orange Punch from his feet. He lands a second and third Orange Punch for good measure, and then cradles the champ with a tight clutch to pick up the win!

Winner via pinfall: Orange Cassidy (3)

Mox looks surprised to realize he’s behind in the Gold League now, leaving the ring as Cassidy is given no time to celebrate thanks to an arriving Nick Wayne. In case you haven’t heard, Nick Wayne…is about to beat “Speedball” Mike Bailey right now! He talks about the two “uncs” at ringside, saying neither of them can say they beat Chris Jericho (pyro pop)…twice. With that, Wayne makes his way toward the ring for our next match as we go to commercial break.

-Commercial Break-

We head back to ringside where we get the special intro for Nick Wayne, before “Speedball” Mike Bailey makes his entrance to get this Continental Classic match underway!

Continental Classic Tournament Gold League Match: ‘Speedball’ Mike Bailey (0) vs. Nick Wayne (0)

Wayne starts mocking Speedball before the two get at it, with Nick taking control as he gets Bailey against the ropes. He mocks the karate-like nature of Speedball with a bow before taking him to the mat, mocking him some more before Speedball takes the Prodigy down. Flurry of offense from both men ends with Wayne being dropped by Mike, who bows to his opponent after Nick mocked him for it earlier.

Nick leaves the ring for a breather (or restmaxxing, depending on who you ask), going back after Speedball upon his return. He hits the ropes for speed but takes a big kick sending him to the canvas. Wayne fires back with some offense, sending Speedball down hard. He brings Bailey back up for a takedown, following Mike to the corner as he brings him back up. Bailey fights back, landing a missile dropkick from the top turnbuckle. Shooting star press from the canvas gets him a two count.

Bailey gets back up top, but Wayne rolls out of harm’s way to the outside. He catches Speedball by surprise on the apron, dropping him to the floor with a Prodigyplex II…but Nick looks like he may have hurt himself in the process as we go to an ad break.

-FULL AD BREAK (ON HBO MAX)-

Both men back in the ring but Wayne gets taken out to the floor with a kick by Speedball, who follows up with a moonsault clearing the top rope. He brings Wayne back into the ring for a shooting star press, but Nick only allows a nearfall…before Mike transitions to a cross arm breaker! Wayne finds a way out of it and goes for Wayne’s World. Bailey avoids the first attempt, but gets caught off the apron with Wayne’s World sending him to the floor instead!

The ref begins the count and both men are down. Nick is the first one to his feet, sliding into the ring. The count reaches eight as Speedball pops up to return to the ring with ten minutes remaining. Nick goes after Speedball again, but gets cradled for two by Bailey…who follows up with a hook kick sending him to the corner. Wayne catches him with a kick but it’s only good for a one count! Nick sent to the corner for Time Adventure by Speedball, who goes for the shooting star…only to get caught with a cutter!

Nick with the cover but only gets a nearfall. Bailey fires back for a two count of his own, only for Nick to roll through for a tight roll up (with a little bit of tightsmaxxing, if you will) to score the win and three points in his first Continental Classic appearance!

Winner via pinfall: Nick Wayne (3)

Nick celebrates his win as we get a video from Darby Allin, who warns Kyle Fletcher and Wardlow that he’s coming after them soon. From there we go backstage, where Don Callis addresses that message as well as Ospreay’s words to Fletcher. Despite taking a couple L’s at the pay-per-view, Callis focuses on the Okada win before addressing that Kevin Knight is the chosen member of the Family for this year’s Continental Classic. Knight faces Ciampa at Collision in tournament action, and Kevin feels confident in his chances.

Callis says he hasn’t forgotten about Kyle Fletcher, promising he won’t be fooled by what Ospreay has to say. Fletcher doesn’t seem too enthused by the remarks as Don says that one thing matters above all…with Okada finishing the sentence: “Family!”

-Commercial Break-

Backstage, the Young Bucks are interviewed about the arrival of the Kasper Brothers…but they’re interrupted by Motor City Machine Guns. They talk about being the number one contenders for the tag titles before talking to the Bucks about working together to deal with FTR and the Kasper Brothers. The champs accept and shake hands as we hear from FTR and the Kasper Brothers. Big Stoke addresses the challenge, hyping up his own past history with the newcomers.

He addresses how great the brothers are while also remembering we have one of the greatest tag teams of all time in FTR, and they accept the challenge. Top guys…out.

We head to ringside as Stoke accompanies the Kasper Brothers to the ring, with their opponents already in the ring to get this match underway!

Kasper Brothers vs. Local Talents

Jacob starts things out by throwing his opponent around, and a double knee to the back from Drew helps things out. Tags are made as the Kaspers continue to go to work on their opponents, leading to a Kasper Cannonball for the win.

Winners via pinfall: Kasper Brothers

The Kaspers celebrate their victory as we head backstage to hear from the new women’s tag champions, Wrathlander. They are excited about taking on new challengers, interrupted by Billy Gunn who get shouted down by the champs before leaving. Wrathlander are then confronted by Divine Dominion, who look to get a match for the titles after noting that the champs have yet to beat them outright.

Stat accepts the challenge, with the Dominion walking away as Dynamite rolls on. We head to ringside as MJF’s music hits, with the “One True Champion” making his way to the ring to boos. He plugs his upcoming film Violent Night 2 before talking about Will Ospreay, running the champ down for his performance since becoming champion. He mocks Ospreay for talking about “getting the feeling back,” asking where that feeling is before declaring himself the feeling.

He offers some facts for Will: reminding us that MJF (technically) kicked out of the One Winged Angel (ignoring how long it took for a count to be made thanks to the ref being knocked out for a while), and pointing out that “the world” doesn’t consider Ospreay the best in the world, unfurling a banner of the PWI cover declaring him the number one wrestler.

He then brings up their past matches, including the “easy dub” with a second remaining, and how he nearly had Ospreay beat before there was outside interference. He then talks about being “screwed” out of facing Ospreay at All In London this past year when Will’s interference at Beach Break lead to him losing to Kenny Omega. He then talks about how Ospreay stole everything from him, before saying Will is NOT the AEW World Champion…MJF is. He holds up his title, begging Ospreay to prove him wrong. He suggests they both put their titles on the line at Worlds End, and MJF shows that he is undisputedly the One True Champion.

He drops the mic as his music plays, and we see Excalibur and Tony Schiavone decked out to talk about the upcoming NHL Winter Classic between the Colorado Avalanche and Utah Mammoth.

We go backstage, where the Death Riders addresses what they’ve been through as of late. They also talk about the Continental Classic, even as we notice a frustrated Mox in the background after losing his match earlier tonight. It’s still Death Riders against the world, and they don’t plan on losing.

We head back to ringside as Samoa Joe makes his entrance to a pop from the crowd. We see Jack Perry at the go position, stabbing an Opps Dojo shirt while wearing a leather jacket with “KILL ME” on the back. His music hits as Perry heads to the ring, and we get this match underway!

Jack Perry vs. Samoa Joe

Perry charges at Joe, who sends him back to the opposite side. Jack fights back before being sent to the outside with a right hand by Joe, who follows him outside for a chop. Perry slides under the ring to escape Joe’s grasp, and Joe demands the ref find him under the ring. Ref goes under the ring just as Perry comes out with a fire extinguisher, trying to cool Joe down while the ref isn’t around to see it.

Joe’s response? Stopping him on the barricade before swinging him into the LED board. Ref admonishes him as Joe slides into the ring, only to leave again to go after the former Jungle Boy with some hard shots to the head. We see Perry is busted open as Joe sends him into the announce table before we go to break.
