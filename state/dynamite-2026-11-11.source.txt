AEW Dynamite/Collision Results 
 November 11, 2026 
 Maverick Center in West Valley City, (Salt Lake City) Utah 
 Results by: Alex Kahrs and Jerome Wilen of Wrestleview.com

Commentary welcomes us to the show before running down the lineup for tonight and Full Gear. From there we go to a video package looking back at the highlights Blood and Guts last week. From Julia Hart submitting to end the women’s B&G match to Cope being forced to surrender in the men’s match, only for Darby Allin to be shoved off the cage and through a stack of tables by Wardlow.

We go to footage from after the show, where Will Ospreay breaks the silence by trying to understand why Cope would break the oath the squad made to not surrender or submit. He runs down Adam by noting the kids watching him in his shirt, and whether he thought Darby wouldn’t be thrown off the cage–but Cope interrupts by addressing his own past with injury, saying he didn’t want to see that for anyone else. He speaks on the injuries the rest of the squad has suffered, and was trying to keep them from having to retire. If they can’t understand that, maybe this isn’t the place for him. He storms off as we head back to ringside.

Will Ospreay and Kyle Fletcher go Face-To-Face

Don Callis accompanies Kyle Fletcher to the ring as commentary talks about his match for the AEW World Championship at Full Gear. Kyle gets a microphone, and out next is Will Ospreay to a big pop from the crowd. As the champ enters the ring to pose for the fans, Kyle says to cut the music. He’s not out here to listen to this BS. This gets boos and “Ospreay” chants from the fans.

The chants turn to “f–k Don Callis,” to which Kyle asks if they’re done. He turns his attention to Ospreay, saying it’s been two years since they had a face-to-face conversation and they’re well due another. He reminds us of when he shaved his head, saying so much has happened…as the crowd tells him “you look stupid.” Kyle disagrees, realizing this is not an argument worth having as he addresses what has happened to Ospreay in that same timeframe. He got married, he got neck surgery, he came back from neck surgery, and he won the AEW World Champion.

This gets “he’s World Champion” chants, to which Fletcher congratulates the crowd on being observant before bringing up his own success in that timeframe. He wraps it up by giving Ospreay some respect and thanks. Ospreay is surprised by this, thanking Kyle and saying he’s kept his eye on him for two years…and he’s proud of Kyle. Fletcher said he is grateful for everything that’s happened since, and this is a point Kyle reiterates as he explains that he would do everything again if he could.

He comes back around to the neck injury that took Ospreay out for months…upset that Ospreay went after Jon Moxley when he came back, when it was Fletcher who dropped him on his neck over and over to take him out. He wants the credit he deserves.

Ospreay admits that it was Kyle who broke his neck, addressing the pain he went through before explaining that the roar of the crowd is what kept him going when he was basically wrestling with one arm. It’s a painkiller for him, bruv. He talks about the bit that Kyle cut off moments ago, saying it’s a call to arms like the Batsignal for him. He then reminds Fletcher that even with a broken neck he beat him twice…and now, fully healed, he is wrestling at a level nobody can replicated.

He talks about outlasting seven opponents in Clockwork Carnage, beating the ace of AEW, hit a Black Arrow when the world needed it most, and beat Kenny Omega for the World Title at All In. He directs the production truck to play a clip he sent in this morning, reminding us that the last two men to see him backstage before he went out where Fletcher and Davis. He asks Kyle to tell him what he was feeling in that moment. Fletcher starts to bring up Don Callis, but Ospreay says he doesn’t want to hear Don’s words, he wants Kyle’s answer. As this goes on, there is a fan yelling out that Fletcher felt jealous, while Ospreay thinks Fletcher and Davis wanted to go out there with him like old times.

Eventually, Fletcher confirms that one fan’s theory by telling the champ that he was jealous. He was jealous that the man he hated most was about to win the biggest prize…until he realized that he had a chance to be the one to take it all away. Ospreay doesn’t believe him, saying he thinks his old pals wanted to be at ringside just like he believes deep down Fletcher still loves him. Kyle claims he never loved Will, and that he and Davis saw the truth about Ospreay. Callum Newman recently saw it, and someday everyone Ospreay ever loved will see it…including Will’s wife and son, and if his son won’t see it on his own, Fletcher will be there to tell him what kind of man Ospreay really is.

Ospreay notes these will be his final comments and then he will see Fletcher on Saturday, giving his challenger credit for his success but saying the two men are not on equal footing. He talks about everything he’s done in this business, and how he will show Fletcher what he showed Moxley, because he is Will Ospreay, and he is on another–

Fletcher slaps the champ in the face, telling him to do something about it and show him that killer instinct. Instead, Will retrieves the mic and finishes his sentence with “level” before leaving the ring as his music plays. With that, we go to a video package hyping up a dream match for Full Gear, as Adam Copeland challenges Kazuchika Okada for the AEW International Championship!

Back at ringside, The New Level make their entrance for our opening contest. Brodido come out next, but Creed and Kofi go after them to get things underway!

The New Level (Austin Creed and Kofi) vs. Brodido (Bandido and Brody King)

All four men go at it at ringside until Brody finally brings Creed to the ring for the opening bell to sound, but Creed gets King to the outside for a dive through the ropes. Bandido rushes into the ring for a tope of his own, but is followed up with a dive from the top turnbuckle by Kofi. He goes around the ring to grab a chair, and encourages Creed to grab a chair of his own as we go to ad break.

-FULL AD BREAK (ON HBO MAX)-

Creed takes down Bandido in the ring but is quickly dropped by Brody, who takes a guillontine leg drop from Kofi. Bandido takes out Kofi, but is caught with a fisherman’s buster by Creed! The New Level stay on top of things until King sends them out of the ring…only to be sent into the ring steps for his troubles as the ref begins the count. They start to celebrate this until Bandido takes them out with a dive to the floor.

He goes after Creed, bringing him up the ramp as he calls to Brody to grab a table…but Kofi attacks him with a chair as we find out both teams have been counted out!

Match results in a Double Count-Out

This barely fazes Brody as he brings the table out, and all four men are going at it. Brody gets sent off the stage and through a table as Creed and Bandio brawl to the back. Security tries to hold back King from going after Kofi as we cut to a video package honoring veterans on this Veterans Day.

Which I will do as well. Happy Veterans Day, and to those who served, thank you for your service!

-Commercial Break-
