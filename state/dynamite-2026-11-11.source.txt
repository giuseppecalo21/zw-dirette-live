AEW Dynamite/Collision Results 
 November 11, 2026 
 Maverick Center in West Valley City, (Salt Lake City) Utah 
 Results by: Alex Kahrs and Jerome Wilen of Wrestleview.com

Commentary welcomes us to the show before running down the lineup for tonight and Full Gear. From there we go to a video package looking back at the highlights Blood and Guts last week. From Julia Hart submitting to end the women’s B&G match to Cope being forced to surrender in the men’s match, only for Darby Allin to be shoved off the cage and through a stack of tables by Wardlow.

We go to footage from after the show, where Will Ospreay breaks the silence by trying to understand why Cope would break the oath the squad made to not surrender or submit. He runs down Adam by noting the kids watching him in his shirt, and whether he thought Darby wouldn’t be thrown off the cage–but Cope interrupts by addressing his own past with injury, saying he didn’t want to see that for anyone else. He speaks on the injuries the rest of the squad has suffered, and was trying to keep them from having to retire. If they can’t understand that, maybe this isn’t the place for him. He storms off as we head back to ringside.

Will Ospreay and Kyle Fletcher go Face-To-Face

Don Callis accompanies Kyle Fletcher to the ring as commentary talks about his match for the AEW World Championship at Full Gear. Kyle gets a microphone, and out next is Will Ospreay to a big pop from the crowd. As the champ enters the ring to pose for the fans, Kyle says to cut the music. He’s not out here to listen to this BS. This gets boos and “Ospreay” chants from the fans.

The chants turn to “f–k Don Callis,” to which Kyle asks if they’re done. He turns his attention to Ospreay, saying it’s been two years since they had a face-to-face conversation and they’re well due another. He reminds us of when he shaved his head, saying so much has happened…as the crowd tells him “you look stupid.” Kyle disagrees, realizing this is not an argument worth having as he addresses what has happened to Ospreay in that same timeframe. He got married, he got neck surgery, he came back from neck surgery, and he won the AEW World Champion.

This gets “he’s World Champion” chants, to which Fletcher congratulates the crowd on being observant before bringing up his own success in that timeframe. He wraps it up by giving Ospreay some respect and thanks. Ospreay is surprised by this, thanking Kyle and saying he’s kept his eye on him for two years…and he’s proud of Kyle. Fletcher said he is grateful for everything that’s happened since, and this is a point Kyle reiterates as he explains that he would do everything again if he could.

He comes back around to the neck injury that took Ospreay out for months…upset that Ospreay went after Jon Moxley when he came back, when it was Fletcher who dropped him on his neck over and over to take him out. He wants the credit he deserves.

Ospreay admits that it was Kyle who broke his neck, addressing the pain he went through before explaining that the roar of the crowd is what kept him going when he was basically wrestling with one arm. It’s a painkiller for him, bruv. He talks about the bit that Kyle cut off moments ago, saying it’s a call to arms like the Batsignal for him. He then reminds Fletcher that even with a broken neck he beat him twice…and now, fully healed, he is wrestling at a level nobody can replicated.

He talks about outlasting seven opponents in Clockwork Carnage, beating the ace of AEW, hit a Black Arrow when the world needed it most, and beat Kenny Omega for the World Title at All In. He directs the production truck to play a clip he sent in this morning, reminding us that the last two men to see him backstage before he went out where Fletcher and Davis. He asks Kyle to tell him what he was feeling in that moment. Fletcher starts to bring up Don Callis, but Ospreay says he doesn’t want to hear Don’s words, he wants Kyle’s answer. As this goes on, there is a fan yelling out that Fletcher felt jealous, while Ospreay thinks Fletcher and Davis wanted to go out there with him like old times.

Eventually, Fletcher confirms that one fan’s theory by telling the champ that he was jealous. He was jealous that the man he hated most was about to win the biggest prize…until he realized that he had a chance to be the one to take it all away. Ospreay doesn’t believe him, saying he thinks his old pals wanted to be at ringside just like he believes deep down Fletcher still loves him. Kyle claims he never loved Will, and that he and Davis saw the truth about Ospreay. Callum Newman recently saw it, and someday everyone Ospreay ever loved will see it…including Will’s wife and son, and if his son won’t see it on his own, Fletcher will be there to tell him what kind of man Ospreay really is.

Ospreay notes these will be his final comments and then he will see Fletcher on Saturday, giving his challenger credit for his success but saying the two men are not on equal footing. He talks about everything he’s done in this business, and how he will show Fletcher what he showed Moxley, because he is Will Ospreay, and he is on another–

Fletcher slaps the champ in the face, telling him to do something about it and show him that killer instinct. Instead, Will retrieves the mic and finishes his sentence with “level” before leaving the ring as his music plays. With that, we go to a video package hyping up a dream match for Full Gear, as Adam Copeland challenges Kazuchika Okada for the AEW International Championship!

Back at ringside, The New Level make their entrance for our opening contest. Brodido come out next, but Creed and Kofi go after them to get things underway!

The New Level (Austin Creed and Kofi) vs. Brodido (Bandido and Brody King)

All four men go at it at ringside until Brody finally brings Creed to the ring for the opening bell to sound, but Creed gets King to the outside for a dive through the ropes. Bandido rushes into the ring for a tope of his own, but is followed up with a dive from the top turnbuckle by Kofi. He goes around the ring to grab a chair, and encourages Creed to grab a chair of his own as we go to ad break.

-FULL AD BREAK (ON HBO MAX)-

Creed takes down Bandido in the ring but is quickly dropped by Brody, who takes a guillontine leg drop from Kofi. Bandido takes out Kofi, but is caught with a fisherman’s buster by Creed! The New Level stay on top of things until King sends them out of the ring…only to be sent into the ring steps for his troubles as the ref begins the count. They start to celebrate this until Bandido takes them out with a dive to the floor.

He goes after Creed, bringing him up the ramp as he calls to Brody to grab a table…but Kofi attacks him with a chair as we find out both teams have been counted out!

Match results in a Double Count-Out

This barely fazes Brody as he brings the table out, and all four men are going at it. Brody gets sent off the stage and through a table as Creed and Bandio brawl to the back. Security tries to hold back King from going after Kofi as we cut to a video package honoring veterans on this Veterans Day.

Which I will do as well. Happy Veterans Day, and to those who served, thank you for your service!

-Commercial Break-

We go to footage from after Blood and Guts last week, with WrathLander and the Brawling Birds having some words about what went down as they gear up for their Women’s World Tag Team Championship match at Full Gear this weekend.

Back at ringside, Orange Cassidy makes his entrance for our next match. David Finlay makes his way out next as commentary talks about what happened to Juice Robinson last Saturday, and the bell rings to get this match underway!

Everyone Banned from Ringside: Orange Cassidy vs. David Finlay

Orange shows he is the smartest wrestler alive as he leaves the ring, walking to the corner where David set down his shillelagh…and tucks it underneath the ring apron to get it out of play! The bell rings after Cassidy gets back in the ring, but Finlay starts to show he doesn’t need that weapon as he goes to work on the former International Champion before Cassidy rolls out of harm’s way with his hands in his pockets.

He comes back just as Finlay leaves the ring, playing a bit of possum with David to keep him off his game. This leads to both men out of the ring as Cassidy finally catches Finlay with a dive, bringing him back in the ring before climbing the top rope…before getting caught in midair by Finlay for a backbreaker, dropping Orange down hard as we go to an ad break!

-FULL AD BREAK (ON HBO MAX)-

Finlay continues to wear down Cassidy, who fires back with his hands in his pockets. Finlay starts to mock him for this before laying Cassidy out with a rolling elbow. Orange starts to counter a suplex, eventually turning it around for one of his own sending Finlay to the mat. He dodges David in the corner, slamming Finlay against the turnbuckle on two corners. Finlay blocks a DDT, but takes a Stundog Millionaire instead. This allows Cassidy to connect with a DDT before going up top…where Finlay catches him with a bite on the head to stop his momentum!

Cassidy returns the favor before knocking Finlay down for a diving DDT to the canvas…but Finlay manages to roll out of harm’s way. He reaches under the ring, pulling out a chair to distract the ref so he can use his shillelagh to level Cassidy for the win.

Winner via pinfall: David Finlay

The ref notices the shillelagh and has it out with Finlay, trying to figure out if he used it to secure the victory. Eventually, David’s arm is raised in victory when The Cut make their way to ringside, only to be cut off by The Conglomeration.

Commentary hypes up the rest of the lineup for tonight before we go backstage, where we hear from Andrade El Idolo. He talks about losing the National Championship at Collision. He admits it was a setback but also points out that when one door closes, another opens. He looks to make history by becoming the first Latino AEW World Champion. This is a promise from Andrade El Idolo, the real Latino man.

Tommaso Ciampa defends the National Title he won from El Idolo later tonight!

We cut to a video hyping up AEW Thanksgiving Eve Dynamite and Thanksgiving Collision on November 25 and 26, respectively.

-Commercial Break-

Backstage, we hear from Stokely ahead of the deciding match in the Best of 5 Series between FTR and the Motor City Machine Guns. We learn that the winners of this series will face whoever leaves Full Gear as the World Tag Team Champions, with Stoke promising that FTR are going to win this series and go on to become four-time tag champions.

Back at ringside, Dr. Britt Baker accompanies Persephone to the ring for our next match. Gypsy Mac is already in the ring as this match gets underway!

Persephone vs. Gypsy Mac

Persephone immediately goes to work on Mac, wearing her down before Mac starts to fight back from the corner…but a big lariat and a bigger powerbomb takes Mac out as Persephone gets the win.

Winner via pinfall: Persephone

Persephone leaves the ring to shout at commentary over her TBS Title being “stolen” by Queen Aminata, who defends it in a 3-Way Match later tonight. Backstage, we hear from Hangman Adam Page as he addresses the agreement for the World Trios Title rematch at Full Gear being Texas Death. Page talks about his history with Swerve Strickland and the fact Swerve demanded this match be Texas Death. He says that going into this match is going in not knowing if you walk back out again. This match is meant to be for the trios titles but Page knows this is about something more.

Page has resisted and told everyone else not to give into the demands by Swerve, but Hangman admits this is about something more than him. Brodido joins him as he says his partners want Texas Death…they need Texas Death. He will walk side by side with his brothers into the mouth of hell.

King understands Adam’s hesitation, but he must know that for years hate was Brody’s worth. Bandido helped him learn a new path, but Texas Death will let him embrace his old self. In Texas Death, you may pray to God that you will survive…with Brody admitting he doesn’t believe in God, but he does believe in violence. Bandido tells New Level and Swerve Strickland to meet their fate at Full Gear.

With that, we head to ringside for our next match. FTR make their entrance first as commentary talks about how they tied up the series at Collision, and the importance of this deciding match in the Best of 5 Series. Out next are the Motor City Machine Guns, and we get this match underway!

Match 5 of Best of Five Series (Series Tied 2-2): FTR (Dax Harwood and Cash Wheeler) vs. Motor City Machine Guns (Alex Shelley and Chris Sabin)

We seem to start with Dax and Sabin until some issues arise between both teams. Instead, we get Shelley and Harwood starting off. Dax with a side headlock takedown before hitting the ropes, rolling up Shelley for a two count. Alex fires back to get Harwood in an armbar, but Dax is back up for a tag to Wheeler. Double-team from FTR to no avail as they get taken to the mat by Shelley, who wrenches the arm of Cash.

Wheeler breaks free as things break down with all four men going at it on the outside as we go to break.

-FULL AD BREAK (ON HBO MAX)-

Wheeler has Shelley in the corner for some right hands, but is caught with a Flatliner into the turnbuckle. Dax with a tag to keep him from going for a tag of his own, only to be sent to the corner himself. Tag is made but with the ref distracted he calls it off, while Dax and Cash pull Shelley away for some more shots. Shelley fights them off and gets the tag to Sabin, who goes at it with Dax.

Blind tag by Wheeler, but FTR are taken down with a flurry of offense by Sabin. He sends Dax out before dropping Cash to the canvas for a nearfall. Wheeler starts to fight back as we get a dueling chant for both teams, and the fight makes it to the corner where Sabin is attacked with a right hand. Tag to Dax, but he gets caught with a cradle for two! The fight spills outside once more as Wheeler is stopped by Shelley from using a ring bell. Dax kicks out of a pin attempt, sending Sabin into a ring bell shot by Cash.

Cover by Harwood as we get a ten minute announcement, but Sabin manages to kick out at the count of two as we go to ad break.

-FULL AD BREAK (ON HBO MAX)-

Things break down as we see all four men at it in the ring, with Stoke attempting to help out only for Sabin and Wheeler to clear out of the ring. Backslide by Harwood only gets a nearfall. He fires away with some shots on Shelley, who lands some of his own in response.

We get a five minute warning as Sabin gets involved as well, with Wheeler looking for a rolling suplex only to be stopped by the Guns. They fire up on Harwood with a double-team and stop Stokely from getting involved. They drop Harwood with a double team neckbreaker for the cover, but Wheeler narrowly breaks it up to save the match!

Harwood turns things around as he catches Shelley with a pin. Stokely helps with some leverage, but Alex shoves Dax off to break the pin for himself. He stares down Stoke who backs off, and FTR hit a Shatter Machine for the cover…but Sabin breaks it up! Stoke gets on the apron for a distraction, but takes a Cradle Shock for his troubles. FTR with some stuffed piledrivers on Sabin and Shelley, but it’s not enough as Shelley kicks out at two to save the match once again.

“Fight Forever” chants from the crowd with one minute remaining. Shelley drops down to stop a stuffed piledriver attempt. Thirty seconds as FTR struggle to set up the piledriver, and Shelley blocks a Shatter Machine. He kicks off Wheeler and finishes a sunset flip for the pin…and with seconds to go gets the win!

Winners via pinfall (winning the series 3-2): Motor City Machine Guns

MCMG celebrate the win as FTR stare them down, stunned by the result. We now know the Guns will face either the Young Bucks or Davis and Doyle after Full Gear, as we see FTR shaking hands with MCMG before leaving the ring. We take a look back at AEW’s history in Salt Lake City before looking ahead to Full Gear this weekend in Phoenix.

Back at ringside, “Speedball” Mike Bailey makes his entrance for our next match as he challenges for the AEW National Championship. Out next is the champion as Tommaso Ciampa makes his entrance, while we get a look at Ciampa winning the title at Collision just as the rain started coming down in Miami. With that, we get this title match underway!

AEW National Championship: Tommaso Ciampa defends against ‘Speedball’ Mike Bailey

The two charge at each other before Ciampa fires off some strikes, only for Speedball to return the favor and send the champ (uh) to the corner. Tommaso responds by sending Bailey to the outside, declaring “Ciampa rules” before leaving the ring to bring Bailey back. Bailey dodges him and hits the ropes, dodging him again to send the champ to the floor with a sky twister press.

Bailey brings Tommaso back into the ring, following him right back out for a kick to the chest. He brings Ciampa back in, but the champ catches him off the ropes before sending Bailey back out…and then into the ring steps, before reminding us that Ciampa Rules. He gives a couple fans a high five for agreeing, and we see Bailey is busted open as we go to ad break.

-FULL AD BREAK (ON HBO MAX)-

Ciampa has his challenger back in the ring, but Bailey turns things around with a release German suplex. He goes to the nearest turnbuckle but out goes Ciampa, who cuts him off at the pass. They fight on the turnbuckle as Bailey tries to go for a sunset powerbomb onto the apron, but to no avail. He connects with a thrust kick instead, before Tommaso catches him with an Air Raid Crash on the hardest part of the ring!

He brings Speedball back in the ring, connecting with a Project Ciampa for the cover…but Bailey kicks out at two! Bailey drops to evade a running knee, but also seems to be out as the ref checks on him.
