WWE Raw Results
November 16, 2026
Raleigh, North Carolina (Lenovo Center)
Commentary: Joe Tessitore and Wade Barrett
Results by: Mike Tedesco of Wrestleview.com

“Then. Now. Forever. Together.” The WWE video leads us into the show.

We’re only 12 days away from Survivor Series. John Cena will be live on Raw tonight to talk about the John Cena Classic. The Judgment Day, IYO SKY, and Stephanie Vaquer are shown arriving.

Parking Garage Segment

A black SUV drives into the parking garage, and The Bloodline, minus Roman Reigns, exits. Raw General Manager Adam Pearce, flanked by two security guards, comes up and says he knows Jacob Fatu wants a piece of 946. If anyone other than Cruz Montana, Carmelo Hayes, or The Usos gets involved in the match tonight, the advantage goes to the other team. Pearce says that if they want the 946, they should win the WarGames Advantage Match and WarGames. Fatu isn’t happy.

The next member of Team USA is announced

“Wrestling has more than one royal family.”

“The American Nightmare” Cody Rhodes heads to the ring to a big ovation. Rhodes greets Raleigh, North Carolina, and asks what they want to talk about. Rhodes says at Survivor Series, Team Mexico, led by WWE Hall of Famer Rey Mysterio, will take on Team USA. A “USA” chant fires up. Rhodes says he’s lucky to be the captain of Team USA. Rhodes wants to announce the team’s fourth member.

CM Punk , who is already on Team USA, heads to the ring. Rhodes looks surprised to see him. Rhodes says we’re 10 minutes into the show and surprises always happen. What is Punk doing here? Punk says he wanted to ask the people if it’s good to be alive on a Monday night in Raleigh or what? The crowd cheers. Punk says he knows he should have let Rhodes know, but he wanted to stand next to Rhodes as Co-Captain as they announce the fourth member of Team USA. Rhodes is confused about the Co-Captain title and says he’s the captain. Punk sounds like he has a cold and asks what’s better than one captain.

The Miz heads to the ring with Kit Wilson . Miz mentions that he, Punk, and Rhodes all debuted close to each other. 20 years later, who is still fighting at their level? Imagine what they can do together. Miz wants to be the fourth member of Team USA. Punk whispers to Rhodes, and they look around. The crowd is mixed. Punk says they’ve already chosen somebody else. Miz is not happy. Rhodes asks, “ We have?” Rhodes asks if Miz has anything else to say. Miz says when he steps into a WWE ring, there is no one better than him. Miz is better than Rhodes and Punk, as well as everyone in the arena. Miz is the best the WWE has to offer. Miz shouts that he deserves this. Half the crowd agrees with him. Punk mentions that Miz dressed up as the Calgary Kid once, which disqualifies him from being on Team USA.

Rhodes asks Punk why he keeps saying “we picked.” Does Punk know who Rhodes picked? Punk sounds uncertain as he says, “Yeah.”

LA Knight heads to the ring to a great ovation. Knight gets in the ring, but Miz interrupts him. Miz is furious. Miz mentions that he’s the first two-time Grand Slam winner. You want a real American? Miz hosts American Gladiators. Miz has starred in several Marine movies. He’s got a hot, sexy wife and is the epitome of the American Dream. You can just call him “Hacksaw” Mike Mizaanin. Knight tells Miz to calm down and talks with Rhodes and Punk. Knight says Miz made a lot of points. Knight mentions that Miz’s DVDs sold less than any DVD ever. Knight won’t give Miz his flowers; he’ll let Miz take them. Knight disrobes, wearing his wrestling trunks. Knight says he’ll let Miz fight him for the spot. Miz looks disgusted.

Referee Danilo Anfibio runs down for this match.

-Commercial Break-

Cody Rhodes and CM Punk are on commentary for this match. The winner will join Team USA.

The Miz w/ Kit Wilson vs. LA Knight

Referee: Danilo Anfibio

We join this match in progress. Miz boots Knight back and charges, but Knight hits a snap powerslam for a two-count. Miz tries to fight back, but Knight hits a neckbreaker for a two-count. Knight stomps Miz in the corner, so Miz goes to the apron and snaps him off the top rope. Miz goes for a roll-up, but Knight sits on his shoulders. Miz pulls him down for a two-count. Knight quickly hits a clothesline. Knight shoulders Miz in the midsection before having a whip reversed on him. Miz distracts the referee, and Wilson grabs Knight. Miz attacks Knight from behind and stomps him. Miz picks up a two-count. Miz shouts to Rhodes and Punk. Miz starts impersonating Dusty Rhodes with the stinging jabs. Miz goes for a Bionic Elbow, but Knight blocks it and sends Miz over the top rope. Knight then crushes Miz with a baseball slide. Knight scares Wilson away and bounces Miz’s face off the commentary table. Knight sends Miz into the ring, and the referee checks on him. Wilson then sends Knight headfirst into the ring post.

-Commercial Break-

Back from the break, Miz and Knight are trading punches, with Knight coming out on top. Miz reverses a whip into the corner and goes for his trademark corner clothesline, but Knight avoids it and hits a neckbreaker. Knight hits a powerslam and gets the crowd going. Knight hits an elbow drop with some theatrics for a two-count. Knight goes for Blunt Force Trauma, but Miz fights out and hits a kneeling DDT for a near fall.

Miz signals for a Skull Crushing Finale, but Knight counters into a roll-up for a two-count. Miz rolls him up and holds the tights for a two-count. Knight boots Miz down and clotheslines Wilson off the apron. Miz hits the Skull Crushing Finale! 1… 2… Knight kicks out! Miz sets up for a Go to Sleep, but Knight slides off. Miz goes for a Skull Crushing Finale, but Knight slips out and hits the BFT for the win.

Winner by Pinfall: LA Knight

LA Knight is officially the fourth member of Team USA. Kit Wilson gets in the ring and yells at Knight. Cody Rhodes and CM Punk get into the ring behind him. Punk hits Wilson with a GTS, and Rhodes finishes him off with a Cross Rhodes. Rhodes, Punk, and Knight celebrate in the ring. Je’Von Evans is also on the team, but he isn’t in the ring with them.

Wendy’s Made to Order Moment: Last week on Raw , World Heavyweight Champion Roman Reigns met Royce Keys in the ring. The Bloodline and 946 collided. Reigns then challenged them to a WarGames match.

The Usos will battle Carmelo Hayes and Cruz Montana of The 946 in a WarGames Advantage Match.

John Cena will be out next.

-Commercial Break-

John Cena reveals the next invitee to the John Cena Classic

John Cena makes his way to the ring to an enormous pop. Cena tells Stew, the cameraman, that he’s got some spring in his step, but he only has so many minutes. The John Cena Championship is in the ring.

So far, invitees to the John Cena Classic on WWE Raw on December 14 are Cody Rhodes, Charlotte Flair, Fatal Influence, Zilla Fatu, Kelani Jordan, Romeo Moreno, and Noam Dar.

The crowd chants for Cena. Cena says they started the night a little quiet, but he can tell they’re ready to go. Cena holds up the title and says, “The Championship is here.” Cena says they know some of the who, the when, and the where. Now, it’s time for the what. Cena says this isn’t a tournament. It’s an evening of opportunity. When it comes to opportunity, especially the John Cena Championship, the people who matter most to him are the fans. They’re the reasons the title exists. When he got to the WWE, he was struggling and almost fired. Cena was trying to be a good employee and didn’t listen to them or their noise. They didn’t care about him. When they told him that they were letting him go, Cena figured he’d go down swinging. Cena put on a backward hat, and they made a little noise, giving him an opportunity. Cena kept doing things, and they kept giving him opportunities. When Cena put his heart on this canvas, they cared. That gave him a chance. Cena says they gave him a chance to live a dream.

Cena says they were ready to riot at ECW One Night Stand. Even the stingiest marks rose from their seats when he was the surprise entrant in the Royal Rumble. They were always honest with him, and Cena starts a dueling chant. Cena says at the end, there was love, and the Retirement Tour was the best year of his life. They wouldn’t even let him turn heel because they know him. They let him know the heel turn didn’t work. If he puts his name on an event, he’ll make sure their voices aren’t forgotten.

The next invitee into the John Cena Classic is… Women’s World Champion Liv Morgan ! Cena says that the Champ is here. Morgan even sings her own theme song. Cena tells her not to ignore the boos because they’re trying to tell her something. They’ve walked the same path. They started as fans, and the business found them by accident. They both almost lost it. Then, Morgan had the bravery to come out and put her heart on the canvas, leading people to care about her. When they care, they’ll be honest; they’ll tell her loud and proud. Cena didn’t invite her because she’s a legacy wrestler. She’s like him: a longshot. Professional wrestling loves longshots. Morgan is proof that fan reaction works. If you kick ass, the fans will make sure she gets another chance. Cena says, “I need you in the Classic.” She shakes his hand, and they embrace.

Morgan takes the John Cena Championship and holds it up with the Women’s World Championship. Cena leaves the ring.

“The Man” Becky Lynch heads to the ring and greets the crowd in Raleigh. Lynch says that John Cena is right because blonde dopes like Morgan do always get to the top. Lynch has made a career of knocking women like her off the top. Morgan says Lynch is jealous of her because John Cena chose her for the Classic instead of Lynch. Cena chose Morgan because she’s the greatest Women’s World Champion of allllll time!

Lynch says not according to Sports Illustrated. Lynch doesn’t need opportunities handed to her. Everything she’s gotten in this company has been done on her own, just like John Cena. Everything Morgan has is off the backs of other people’s hard work. “Dirty” Dominik Mysterio was on track to being the face of the company when Morgan sucked the life out of him. The most talented person in Morgan’s group, Roxanne Perez, is the only one not holding gold. Perez was meant to be a Prodigy, but Morgan is holding her back. That Intercontinental Women’s Championship Lynch put a shine on has dimmed because Raquel Rodriguez is being used to prop up Morgan’s championship reign. Morgan isn’t a champion. She’s a parasite. Morgan isn’t the face of the company. That’s why, at Survivor Series, Lynch needs to take the title back. People can say a lot about her, but no one can say she doesn’t try hard in the ring. Morgan makes everyone worse.

John Cena sees something in Morgan. Let’s prove Lynch wrong. Lynch doesn’t want to wait until Survivor Series. She’ll give Morgan the first shot. Morgan declines. Morgan tries to blindside her, but Lynch avoids it and goes for a Manhandle Slam. Morgan quickly gets out of it and scurries away.

Replay: Last Friday on SmackDown , Stephanie Vaquer returned to help Charlotte Flair, Alexa Bliss, Tatum Paxley, and Bayley.

Non-Title Match 
Stephanie Vaquer vs. WWE Women’s United States Champion Jacy Jayne

Referee: Eddie Orengo

The match begins, and Vaquer is evenly matched with Jayne at first. Jayne has a little advantage, but Vaquer fires back with a dropkick and a back suplex. Vaquer sets up for a Devil’s Kiss, but Jayne rolls to the apron. Jayne goes outside and tries to powerbomb Vaquer off the apron. Vaquer avoids it and goes for a knee drop, but she hits the apron when Jayne moves. Jayne takes her down on the floor.

-Commercial Break-

Back from the break, Vaquer hits a jawbreaker, but Jayne quickly takes her down. Vaquer boots her back and sidesteps her before hitting a pair of clotheslines. Vaquer strikes her and hits the ropes, but Jayne follows her in. La Primera fires back with a tilt-a-whirl backbreaker. Vaquer goes for a splash in the corner, but Jayne puts her on the apron. Vaquer goes to the top rope, but Jayne knocks her down. Jayne climbs the ropes and hits a super hurricanrana, followed by a knee to the face for a near fall.

Vaquer avoids a Rolling Encore and (sort of) hits a DDT before rolling her up for a two-count. They fumble around and bungle a spot where Jayne was supposed to block a kick. This match is falling apart. Vaquer hits a dragon screw off the ropes and hits the Devil’s Kiss. Vaquer follows up with a meteora in the corner and goes to the top rope. Fallon Henley and Laney Reid run down and attack Vaquer.

Winner by Disqualification: Stephanie Vaquer

Bayley runs down for the save and hits Reid with a Bayley-to-Belly on the floor. Bayley gets in the ring and takes out Henley. Jayne avoids an elbow drop off the top rope. Fatal Influence starts to attack Bayley, but Vaquer gets up to try to help. Fatal Influence quickly takes Vaquer down. Jayne then hits Bayley with a Rolling Encore. Fatal Influence stands tall.
