WWE Raw Results
November 16, 2026
Raleigh, North Carolina (Lenovo Center)
Commentary: Joe Tessitore and Wade Barrett
Results by: Mike Tedesco of Wrestleview.com

“Then. Now. Forever. Together.” The WWE video leads us into the show.

We’re only 12 days away from Survivor Series. John Cena will be live on Raw tonight to talk about the John Cena Classic. The Judgment Day, IYO SKY, and Stephanie Vaquer are shown arriving.

Parking Garage Segment

A black SUV drives into the parking garage, and The Bloodline, minus Roman Reigns, exits. Raw General Manager Adam Pearce, flanked by two security guards, comes up and says he knows Jacob Fatu wants a piece of 946. If anyone other than Cruz Montana, Carmelo Hayes, or The Usos gets involved in the match tonight, the advantage goes to the other team. Pearce says that if they want the 946, they should win the WarGames Advantage Match and WarGames. Fatu isn’t happy.

The next member of Team USA is announced

“Wrestling has more than one royal family.”

“The American Nightmare” Cody Rhodes heads to the ring to a big ovation. Rhodes greets Raleigh, North Carolina, and asks what they want to talk about. Rhodes says at Survivor Series, Team Mexico, led by WWE Hall of Famer Rey Mysterio, will take on Team USA. A “USA” chant fires up. Rhodes says he’s lucky to be the captain of Team USA. Rhodes wants to announce the team’s fourth member.

CM Punk , who is already on Team USA, heads to the ring. Rhodes looks surprised to see him. Rhodes says we’re 10 minutes into the show and surprises always happen. What is Punk doing here? Punk says he wanted to ask the people if it’s good to be alive on a Monday night in Raleigh or what? The crowd cheers. Punk says he knows he should have let Rhodes know, but he wanted to stand next to Rhodes as Co-Captain as they announce the fourth member of Team USA. Rhodes is confused about the Co-Captain title and says he’s the captain. Punk sounds like he has a cold and asks what’s better than one captain.

The Miz heads to the ring with Kit Wilson . Miz mentions that he, Punk, and Rhodes all debuted close to each other. 20 years later, who is still fighting at their level? Imagine what they can do together. Miz wants to be the fourth member of Team USA. Punk whispers to Rhodes, and they look around. The crowd is mixed. Punk says they’ve already chosen somebody else. Miz is not happy. Rhodes asks, “ We have?” Rhodes asks if Miz has anything else to say. Miz says when he steps into a WWE ring, there is no one better than him. Miz is better than Rhodes and Punk, as well as everyone in the arena. Miz is the best the WWE has to offer. Miz shouts that he deserves this. Half the crowd agrees with him. Punk mentions that Miz dressed up as the Calgary Kid once, which disqualifies him from being on Team USA.

Rhodes asks Punk why he keeps saying “we picked.” Does Punk know who Rhodes picked? Punk sounds uncertain as he says, “Yeah.”

LA Knight heads to the ring to a great ovation. Knight gets in the ring, but Miz interrupts him. Miz is furious. Miz mentions that he’s the first two-time Grand Slam winner. You want a real American? Miz hosts American Gladiators. Miz has starred in several Marine movies. He’s got a hot, sexy wife and is the epitome of the American Dream. You can just call him “Hacksaw” Mike Mizaanin. Knight tells Miz to calm down and talks with Rhodes and Punk. Knight says Miz made a lot of points. Knight mentions that Miz’s DVDs sold less than any DVD ever. Knight won’t give Miz his flowers; he’ll let Miz take them. Knight disrobes, wearing his wrestling trunks. Knight says he’ll let Miz fight him for the spot. Miz looks disgusted.

Referee Danilo Anfibio runs down for this match.

-Commercial Break-

Cody Rhodes and CM Punk are on commentary for this match. The winner will join Team USA.

The Miz w/ Kit Wilson vs. LA Knight

Referee: Danilo Anfibio

We join this match in progress. Miz boots Knight back and charges, but Knight hits a snap powerslam for a two-count. Miz tries to fight back, but Knight hits a neckbreaker for a two-count. Knight stomps Miz in the corner, so Miz goes to the apron and snaps him off the top rope. Miz goes for a roll-up, but Knight sits on his shoulders. Miz pulls him down for a two-count. Knight quickly hits a clothesline. Knight shoulders Miz in the midsection before having a whip reversed on him. Miz distracts the referee, and Wilson grabs Knight. Miz attacks Knight from behind and stomps him. Miz picks up a two-count. Miz shouts to Rhodes and Punk. Miz starts impersonating Dusty Rhodes with the stinging jabs. Miz goes for a Bionic Elbow, but Knight blocks it and sends Miz over the top rope. Knight then crushes Miz with a baseball slide. Knight scares Wilson away and bounces Miz’s face off the commentary table. Knight sends Miz into the ring, and the referee checks on him. Wilson then sends Knight headfirst into the ring post.

-Commercial Break-

Back from the break, Miz and Knight are trading punches, with Knight coming out on top. Miz reverses a whip into the corner and goes for his trademark corner clothesline, but Knight avoids it and hits a neckbreaker. Knight hits a powerslam and gets the crowd going. Knight hits an elbow drop with some theatrics for a two-count. Knight goes for Blunt Force Trauma, but Miz fights out and hits a kneeling DDT for a near fall.

Miz signals for a Skull Crushing Finale, but Knight counters into a roll-up for a two-count. Miz rolls him up and holds the tights for a two-count. Knight boots Miz down and clotheslines Wilson off the apron. Miz hits the Skull Crushing Finale! 1… 2… Knight kicks out! Miz sets up for a Go to Sleep, but Knight slides off. Miz goes for a Skull Crushing Finale, but Knight slips out and hits the BFT for the win.

Winner by Pinfall: LA Knight

LA Knight is officially the fourth member of Team USA. Kit Wilson gets in the ring and yells at Knight. Cody Rhodes and CM Punk get into the ring behind him. Punk hits Wilson with a GTS, and Rhodes finishes him off with a Cross Rhodes. Rhodes, Punk, and Knight celebrate in the ring. Je’Von Evans is also on the team, but he isn’t in the ring with them.

Wendy’s Made to Order Moment: Last week on Raw , World Heavyweight Champion Roman Reigns met Royce Keys in the ring. The Bloodline and 946 collided. Reigns then challenged them to a WarGames match.

The Usos will battle Carmelo Hayes and Cruz Montana of The 946 in a WarGames Advantage Match.

John Cena will be out next.

-Commercial Break-
