AEW Collision Results 
 November 21, 2026 
 Resch Center in Green Bay, Wisconsin 
 Results by: Jerome Wilen of Wrestleview.com

We go live right inside the venue, as Tony Schiavone, Paul Wight and Nigel McGuinness welcome us to the show!

Commentary runs down the matches for tonight’s show.

Tonight’s action starts off with the lights in the venue going dark. The music of Tommaso Ciampa hits, as he makes his way out to the entrance area. Ciampa makes his way down to the ring, but not before stopping to point to a fan in the front row with a Green Bay is All Elite sign.

Next up, the music of Kevin Knight, as he makes his way out and slowly heads down to the ring, wearing blue Bret Hart like sunglasses.

Continental Classic Gold League: Tommaso Ciampa vs. Kevin Knight

The bell rings, as we get a big Ciampa chant from the Green Bay fans. Knight goes to the floor calling for a time out, getting big boos. Both men lock up as Ciampa sends Knight to the ropes. We get a big ‘let’s go Ciampa’ chant. We get a clean break, with both men locking up. Ciampa takes down Knight, going for a quick pin, but its a one count. Knight quickly rolls to the outside, Ciampa slides to the outside, but is met by Knight, kicking him. Ciampa quickly recovers and connects with a big knee on Knight, sending him down on the floor. Ciampa tosses Knight over the barricade. Ciampa grabs him and does it again. Ciampa sends Knight back in the ring and tries for a pin, but its only a two count. Knight grabs Ciampa and drops him face first on the ring apron. Knight misses with a knee. Ciampa attempts a flying knee, as Knight moves, as Ciampa hits the steel steps. Knight sends Ciampa into the barricade. Knight rolls Ciampa back in the ring and connects with a big flying forearm off the top rope. Knight covers Ciampa for a two count. Knight sits Ciampa on the top rope. Knight tries for a superplex, but its stopped by Ciampa who bites Knight. He goes down, then leaps up, but is caught for a psycho driller off the top! Ciampa covers Knight for only a two count. Ciampa is holding his left knee, as the ref checks on him, right before the break.

-FULL AD BREAK (ON HBO MAX)-

Back from the break, Ciampa and Knight exchange open handed slaps. Knight drills Ciampa down and covers him for only a two count. Knight picks up Ciampa, but counters. Both men tries to pick up each other, but block it. Ciampa gets the advantage and drops Knight on his back. Both men exchange blows to the head. Knight connects with a knee on Ciampa, but he come back with a swinging neckbreaker on Knight. Ciampa has Knight up and connects with knees on his back. Ciampa covers Knight, but he won’t stay down! Ciampa exposes his knee, dropping the pad. He charges at Knight, but is caught with a back elbow by Knight. Ciampa misses with a running knee. Knight hits Ciampa with a drop kick, but he stays on his feet. Ciampa nails Knight with a knee and covers him for a two count. The fans chant ‘AEW!’

Ciampa picks up Knight and chops him. Knight goes to the other corner, but Ciampa chops him again. Ciampa puts Knight on the top rope, giving him a big chop in the chest. Ciampa is up on the top. Knight moves and kicks Ciampa in the back of the leg. Knight works on Ciampa left leg, connecting with a coast to coast. Ciampa goes to the floor, as Knight follows. Knight connects with a big fist off the apron. Knight rolls Ciampa back in the ring and comes off the top, getting hit with a knee by Ciampa. He covers Knight for a two count! Ciampa goes for a running knee, but Knight chop blocks Ciampa, attacking his right knee. Knight goes to the top rope. Knight lands on the knee of Ciampa with a UFO Splash. He covers Ciampa for a two count. Knight comes off the top, landing on the top rope instead of Ciampa. Ciampa connects with an exposed knee on Knight, but he kicks out at two! Knight quickly rolls up Ciampa, holding him down or the pin.

Winner by pinfall: Kevin Knight
