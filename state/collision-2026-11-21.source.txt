AEW Collision Results 
 November 21, 2026 
 Resch Center in Green Bay, Wisconsin 
 Results by: Jerome Wilen of Wrestleview.com

We go live right inside the venue, as Tony Schiavone, Paul Wight and Nigel McGuinness welcome us to the show!

Commentary runs down the matches for tonight’s show.

Tonight’s action starts off with the lights in the venue going dark. The music of Tommaso Ciampa hits, as he makes his way out to the entrance area. Ciampa makes his way down to the ring, but not before stopping to point to a fan in the front row with a Green Bay is All Elite sign.

Next up, the music of Kevin Knight, as he makes his way out and slowly heads down to the ring, wearing blue Bret Hart like sunglasses.

Continental Classic Gold League: Tommaso Ciampa vs. Kevin Knight

The bell rings, as we get a big Ciampa chant from the Green Bay fans. Knight goes to the floor calling for a time out, getting big boos. Both men lock up as Ciampa sends Knight to the ropes. We get a big ‘let’s go Ciampa’ chant. We get a clean break, with both men locking up. Ciampa takes down Knight, going for a quick pin, but its a one count. Knight quickly rolls to the outside, Ciampa slides to the outside, but is met by Knight, kicking him. Ciampa quickly recovers and connects with a big knee on Knight, sending him down on the floor. Ciampa tosses Knight over the barricade. Ciampa grabs him and does it again. Ciampa sends Knight back in the ring and tries for a pin, but its only a two count. Knight grabs Ciampa and drops him face first on the ring apron. Knight misses with a knee. Ciampa attempts a flying knee, as Knight moves, as Ciampa hits the steel steps. Knight sends Ciampa into the barricade. Knight rolls Ciampa back in the ring and connects with a big flying forearm off the top rope. Knight covers Ciampa for a two count. Knight sits Ciampa on the top rope. Knight tries for a superplex, but its stopped by Ciampa who bites Knight. He goes down, then leaps up, but is caught for a psycho driller off the top! Ciampa covers Knight for only a two count. Ciampa is holding his left knee, as the ref checks on him, right before the break.

-FULL AD BREAK (ON HBO MAX)-

Back from the break, Ciampa and Knight exchange open handed slaps. Knight drills Ciampa down and covers him for only a two count. Knight picks up Ciampa, but counters. Both men tries to pick up each other, but block it. Ciampa gets the advantage and drops Knight on his back. Both men exchange blows to the head. Knight connects with a knee on Ciampa, but he come back with a swinging neckbreaker on Knight. Ciampa has Knight up and connects with knees on his back. Ciampa covers Knight, but he won’t stay down! Ciampa exposes his knee, dropping the pad. He charges at Knight, but is caught with a back elbow by Knight. Ciampa misses with a running knee. Knight hits Ciampa with a drop kick, but he stays on his feet. Ciampa nails Knight with a knee and covers him for a two count. The fans chant ‘AEW!’

Ciampa picks up Knight and chops him. Knight goes to the other corner, but Ciampa chops him again. Ciampa puts Knight on the top rope, giving him a big chop in the chest. Ciampa is up on the top. Knight moves and kicks Ciampa in the back of the leg. Knight works on Ciampa left leg, connecting with a coast to coast. Ciampa goes to the floor, as Knight follows. Knight connects with a big fist off the apron. Knight rolls Ciampa back in the ring and comes off the top, getting hit with a knee by Ciampa. He covers Knight for a two count! Ciampa goes for a running knee, but Knight chop blocks Ciampa, attacking his right knee. Knight goes to the top rope. Knight lands on the knee of Ciampa with a UFO Splash. He covers Ciampa for a two count. Knight comes off the top, landing on the top rope instead of Ciampa. Ciampa connects with an exposed knee on Knight, but he kicks out at two! Knight quickly rolls up Ciampa, holding him down or the pin.

Winner by pinfall: Kevin Knight

We go a video package recapping the Continental Classic from this past Wednesday’s Dynamite.

Backstage with Wrathlander talking about how they will be scouting Divine Dominion tonight in preparation for their title defense on Dynamite. They will be on commentary for our next match of the night. They make their way out and down to the broadcast table.

Viva Van and VertVixen are out first. The music of Divine Dominion is next, as the former AEW Women’s World Tag Team Champs make their way out and down to the ring.

Divine Dominion (Megan Bayne and Lena Kross) vs. Viva Van and VertVixen

The bell rings, as Bayne and VertVixen start off. Bayne sends Vert to the ropes. Vert hits Bayne in the chest, but she comes back, dropping Vert. Bayne runs the ropes, catches Vert and German Suplexes her. Vert is in the corner of the heels. Divine Dominion double team Vert. Kross is in as Van comes in and is caught and spun around. Kross drops Van on the mat and covers her for a two count. Bayne is as Van chops Bayne. Kross is sent to the floor. Van and Vert double team Bayne. Bayne is sent to the outside. Van attempts to run the through the ropes, but misses and is caught by Bayne and Kross. Bayne and Kross send Vert and Van to the mat with stereo belly to back suplexes.

-FULL AD BREAK (ON HBO MAX)-

Back from the break, the action is back in the ring. Bayne lays into Van. Bayne chops her across the chest. Van comes back with a boot on Bayne. Van connects with a spin kick on Bayne. Kross is in, Vert is tagged in. Vert sends down Kross and then connects with a running pump kick on Kross as she is up in the corner. Vert covers Kross, but kicks out at two. Kross comes back with a reverse kick on Vert. Bayne is tagged in. Kross kicks Vert and then German Suplexes her. Bayne covers Vert, but Van makes the save. Divine Dominion double team Vert. Van is tagged in. Bayne connects on Van with a flying lariat. Van is up and down for a Divine Intervention. Bayne covers Van for the three count.

After the match, Vert comes in the ring and drops Vert with a Divine Intervention. Statlander and Cameron run off Bayne and Kross and check on Vert and Van. Divine Dominion attack them from behind. The Women’s Tag Champs recover, as we get brawl. Security comes out to break things up.

We go backstage with Lexy Nair talking to Persephone and Britt Baker. Persephone is upset about losing the TBS Title. Britt says its time to move on like Aaron Rodgers moved from Green Bay to Pittsburgh. Baker says it might be time for them to move on to other titles.

Back in the venue, the music of Brody King hits, as he makes his way out and down to the ring. Next up is the music of the Death Riders. We get the usual entrance, with Daniel Garcia making his out from the back and through the crowd, before eventually getting into the ring.

Continental Classic Blue League: Brody King vs. Daniel Garcia

The bell rings, as we are underway. Both men lock up, as King sends Garcia to the corner. Garcia ducks a swinging arm from King. Garcia chops King, but it has no affect. King pushes Garcia down. Garcia hits King with a forearm and chop, but King absorbs the chops. Garcia runs the ropes and tries for a running lariat, but King won’t move. Garcia drop kicks King in the legs, but he still stays standing! King chops Garcia. King goes after Garcia, but he moves and lays big rights into the head of King. With one chops, King sends Garcia to the floor! King goes to the outside and chops Garcia over the barricade. King sets up a chair as Garcia sits down on it and then connects on Garcia with a big flying crossbody.

-FULL AD BREAK (ON HBO MAX)-

Back from the break, the action is back in the ring, as Garcia is fired up with both fists up. Garcia does his dance. King swings and misses. Garcia rocks the side of the head of King with big elbow blows to the side of his head. King lands a huge chop on the back of Garcia. He is down in the corner. King connects with a cannonball on Garcia in the corner. The ref checks on Garcia, who is struggling to get up. Garcia has big welts on the back and can’t continue. The bell rings, as King wins by ref stoppage as the doctor stops the fight. Dr. Sampson checks on Garcia while King looks concerned. King takes a knee. The camera stays on King, as he rallies the chants of ‘Garcia’ from the fans. Commentary talked as if this was not planned.

Winner by referee stoppage: Brody King

-COMMERCIAL BREAK-

Back from the break, commentary says Garcia has an arm injury, but will be okay.

The music of El Clon hits, as he makes his way out and down to the ring with RPG Vice.

Next up, the National Champion – IIja Dragonuv. He makes his way out and down to the ring, ready to defend the title.

AEW National Championship Match: IIja Dragonuv defends against El Clon

The bell rings, as this title match is underway. Both men lock up, as Dragonuv takes down Clon with a side headlock. Clon tries to break the hold, but Dragonuv holds on. The hold is broken, as both men lock up. Clon takes down IIja with a headlock, but IIja reverses it for a pin attempt. Clon goes to the rope and back elbows IIja, who comes back with a knee to the mid-section. Clon is on the apron. IIja chops Clon, as he goes for the top rope. Clon uses the ropes to his advantage, stopping on IIja. Clon ties up IIja in the ropes, then chops him, but the champ comes back with a jumping knee strike on Clon. IIja goes to the top rope, but is sent down. IIja comes back with a running head strike on Clon. Clon sends IIja down on the floor, as Clon comes off the apron with a moonsault on the champion.

-FULL AD BREAK (ON HBO MAX)-

Back from the break, the action is back in the ring. Clon kicks IIja in the back. Clon connects with a big kick on IIja, but he fends him off with a knife edge kick. IIja chops Clon in the corner. Clon connects with a kick to the face of IIja. Both men are down, as the fans chant ‘AEW!’ IIja lays elbows into the back of Clon. IIja has Clon in a hold, but Clon sends him into the ropes to break the hold. IIja comes back with multiple German Supelexes on Clon. IIja covers Clon for a two count. IIja grabs Clon from behind, as Clon tries to fight out of the hold, but IIja grabs the arms and German Suplexes Clon. Both men are down. IIja is bleeding from the forehead. IIja runs at Clon, but he comes back with a big kick on IIja, which staggers him. Clon connects with a knee on IIja. Clon covers the champ for only a two count.

IIja has Clon in a headlock, as the challenger tries to fight out of the hold. IIja has Clon on his shoulders and drives him down face first, as both men are down. The ref checks on both men. IIja comes back with a big shot to the side of the head. Both men exchange chops in the center of the ring! IIja lays big fists to the back of Clon. IIja picks him up, but breaks free. Clon kicks IIja, sending the champ down, face first. Both men are down.

IIja is bleeding from the forehead. IIja runs at Clon, but he comes back with a big kick on IIja, which staggers him. Clon connects with a knee on IIja. Clon covers the champ for only a two count.

IIja has Clon in a headlock, as the challenger tries to fight out of the hold. IIja has Clon on his shoulders and drives him down face first, as both men are down. The ref checks on both men. IIja comes back with a big shot to the side of the head. Both men exchange chops in the center of the ring! IIja lays big fists to the back of Clon. IIja picks him up, but breaks free. Clon kicks IIja, sending the champ down, face first. Both men are down. IIja and Clon are up in separate corners. IIja chops Clon, who goes down. IIja comes off the top with a big senton splash on Clon. IIja goes to the top again, but Clon gets a knee up, connecting on IIja! Clon goes to the top rope and comes down with a double stop on the shoulder of IIja! Clon covers the champ, but he kicks out at two!

Clon goes for a D-Bomb, on IIja but misses. IIja picks up Clon, slams him down, stops on his head and then German Suplexes him. IIja connects with a D-Bomb on Clon, then covers him to get the three count to retain the title!

Winner by pinfall and still AEW National Champion: IIja Dragonuv

Back in venue, VPG Vice says IIja Dragonuv is a marked man. Rocky Romero says Andrade will get what he deserves on Wednesday night. Trent says Don Callis is the greatest man alive and that Kyle Fletcher will never leave the Callis Family. Trent says if anyone leaves the family he will beat them right here, right now.

He comes back and nails Takeshita, but he quickly recovers and picks up Trent, dropping him to the mat. Romero distracts Takesshita, allowing Trent to land a DDT on him. Trent picks him up, but Takeshita reverses the hold. Takeshita connects with a styles clash on Trent and then piledrives him hard on the mat. Takeshita connects with a jumping knee on Trent and then drops him with the raging fire for the win.
