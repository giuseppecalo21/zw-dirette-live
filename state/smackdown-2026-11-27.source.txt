WWE SmackDown Results
November 27, 2026
 Wichita, Kansas (Intrust Bank Arena) 
Commentary: Michael Cole and Corey Graves
Results by: Roy Nemer of Wrestleview.com

WWE SmackDown kicks off with clips of Raw where Cody Rhodes announced that Seth Rollins will be on Team USA at the Survivor Series.

In the arena, CM Punk’s music hits and out he comes to the ring. He talks about Thanksgiving and says that happy is not the word he is feeling right now. Punk says his genius captain Cody Rhodes picked Seth Rollins for his team. He says he is not pleased but what is a little shocking is that he is not mad. He says he is disappointed in his friend Cody, annoyed, incredibly. He says sometimes he forgets how much fun this business can be.

He says he had American socks on, his face painted, he was in the ring with Hacksaw Jim Duggan without the pressure of being Champion and now it feels like all of the air has been taken out of the room.

Cody’s music hits and out he comes to the ring. Cody asks him if he wants him to apologize. Punk says he doesn’t have to apologize. Cody tells him that this is someone that revolutionized the business, one of the best of all time and someone who gives them a best time to win. Punk tells him he is crazy for saying that he revolutionized the business and he asks Cody if he is onm crack.

Punk says he wants to win but this is not worth it, this is not the price that he was willing to pay. Cody says for someone who taught him so much in his career why is he the one telling him that this doesn’t look like or sound like CM Punk of old. The CM Punk who holds a grudge for no reason. He says it is no secret that he is adored by the entire locker room, that one day he might run the show, it is no secret.

He says that is the CM Punk he loves and they know. He says he is not trying to make Punk feel better about the situation ,he knows that he has a problem with Seth Rollins. He says he doesn’t want Punk to be mad at Seth, he wants him to be mad at him. Punk says he is mad at Cody and he should be mad at himself for picking Seth. He tells him not to mention anything that Seth has done or said to him in the last 10 years. Punk says LA Knight is not thrilled, he thinks J’Veon maybe is happy to just be there. HE says it’s not just about him, out of the blue, Seth is suddenly patriotic. Punk says Seth is doing this not to win but for one reason and that is to piss him off.

Seth’s music hits and out he comes to the ring with a 2×4 stick. Seth welcomes everyone to the show. Seth says he has missed the fans, he missed being out in the ring, the energy, the burn it down. The fans start to sing his song and Seth asks them to sing it louder for CM Punk. The fans begin to sing. He says it feels good to be needed and to be needed by CM Punk.

Seth tells Punk this is fun, team USA, five on five, Survivor Series match. Seth says he knows what this is about, it’s about the 2×4 isn’t it. He says he picked it up because Punk dropped it on Monday but it means more to Punk than to him. Seth offers Punk the 2×4, Cody grabs it and gives it to Punk. Seth says that’s step one. If it’s not the 2×4, it has to be the co captain situation, that meant a lot to Punk, he says that Cody mentioned to Seth that he can be co-captain since Punk relinquished the spot but he doesn’t need it. He says Punk can be the co-captain.

He says it’s the holiday season, if he wants to have fun, for them to have fun. He asks Punk how the family is, how is AJ and Punk gets in Seth’s face but Cody breaks it up.

LA Knight’s music hits and out he comes to the ring. LA Knight says Seth loves to piss of Punk and Punk loves America, Cody loves to be captain but he doesn’t care. He cares about Survivor Series and they have a mission to complete. He says he wants to be the voice of reason. He tells Punk it’s one night, all he has to do is get through one night. He says if he wants to stomp Seth up, he will watch it himself. LA Knight says he looks at Seth and the best thing about him is his song and what a biblical disaster that is. Punk does LA Knight’s yeah. But Punk has to get through Survivor Series.

He tells Cody that he wants to thank him for bringing him into the fold but at the same time Punk is right, he should be pissed at him. LA Knight says Seth was a piss poor decision. Seth gets in LA Knight’s face and out comes Je’Von Evans to the ring. He says they are fighting for something bigger. He says this is supposed to be fun. He tells Punk that this is the most fun he probably had in his career and he can probably say the same. He is teaming with people who grew up watching.

He is teaming with the mega store, the revolutionary, the American Nightmare and the best in the world. If you ask anyone there, that is a dream team. But it’s a shame that he has to say this, he is the youngest one here and the only one acting like an adult. Cody says that’s an 8 out of 10 on the pep talk. Cody shakes LA Knight’s hand, Je’Von Evans and Seth. He goes to shake Punk’s hand but Punk oooks at him. Seth tells Punk to shake Cody’s hand. He tells Punk not to be a coward, to shake his hand or take his ball and go home again. He says they didn’t need him he walked out 15 years ago and they don’t need him now.

Punk shakes Cody’s hand and says he will see him tomorrow. He drops the microphone and leaves the ring.

A 30 years of The Rock promo airs.

-Commercial Break-

Back from commercial, CM Punk and Cody Rhodes are backstage. Cody tells Punk to take the 2×4 and to keep it. Punk tells him to hold on to it, Cody is the captain. Cody tells him he needs his co-captain. Punk tells Cody he feels like he picked his co-captain. He says he will be there tomorrow, he will watch Cody’s back but for Cody to watch his because his co-captain is a snake.

WWE Women’s Tag Team Championship match – Michin and B-Fab (c’s) with Jade Cargill vs. Alexa Bliss and Tatum Paxley with Charlotte Flair

The bell rings and we are under way. Paxley and Michin start the match. Paxley with a double underhook suplex. She throws Michin to the ropes but B-Fab is tagged in and they double team Paxley. B-Fan with a side slam. She goes for the cover but Paxley kicks out. B-Fab goes for a kick but Paxley moves out of the way. Paxley with a knee to the face and Bliss is tagged in. Michin is tagged in. Bliss with a shoulder tackle. Bliss with a drop kick. Bliss to the ropes but B-Fab trips her up and Michin with a drop kick. Bliss rolls to the outside of the ring and Michin throws Bliss into the barricade.

-Commercial Break-

Back from commercial, B-Fab and Bliss are in the ring. B-Fab with a kick to the face. She grabs Bliss but Bliss with a DDT. Paxley is tagged in and she drop kicks Michin off the ring apron. Paxley with a right hand onto B-Fab. and a kick to the side of the head. She goes for the cover but B-Fab kicks out. Paxley on the ring apron and a kick to the face onto B-Fab. Michin is tagged in. Paxley gets in the ring and she hits B-Fab with a super kick. Bliss is tagged in. Bliss climbs the top rope and she lands Twisted Bliss. She goes for the cover but B-Fab breaks it up. B-Fab throws Paxley to the outside of the ring. B-Fab distracts the referee, Jade gets on the ring apron and hits Bliss with a right hand. Michin with Eat Dafeat. She goes for the cover but Bliss kicks out.

Flair throws Jade over the announce table on the outside of the ring. Paxley throws B-Fab into the ring post. Bliss with Sister Abigail onto Michin. She goes for the cover and gets the pin.

Winners: New WWE Women’s Tag Team Champions, Alexa Bliss and Tatum Paxley

Out come Fatal Influence after the match and they attack Bliss, Flair and Paxley. Out come Bayley and Stephanie Vaquer to the ring. They hit Fatal Influence with right hands but Michin and B-Fab get in the ring and they attack Bayley and Stephanie.

We cut to the back and Byron Saxton is with Kevin Owens. He tells Byron that he is excited about tonight and in comes Finn. He tells Owens that he likes his shirt and Owens is wearing a Finn Balor shirt. Finn shows him his shirt and he is wearing an Owens shirt. Owens tells him he liked his kick to Gunther last week. Finn talks about Sami Zayn kicking Owens and Owens says he can get revenge against Zayn. They shake hands. Owens says he has to go work on his kicks and he grabs Byron to help him.

-Commercial Break-

A video airs of Danhausen’s Thanksgiving. We see Ricky Saints knocking on a door and out comes Chelsea Green. She tells him this is the worst party ever and she walks away. Ricky looks at her confused and he turns around. We see Danhausen and he calls Ricky Richard. Danhausen closes the door and we cut to Cody Rhodes backstage.

He is stopped by LA Knight and Je’Von Evans. Cody tells them that he spoke to Punk and Punk will show up. He tells them that he knows they were trying to help earlier but they were not all that helpful. LA Knight says Cody made the decision he thinks was right but he has to know that he sucked the air out of the room. He says he doesn’t have to worry about him, they will show up and they just have to worry about Punk showing up. Je’Von says this whole thing was supposed to be fun for him and right now it’s not. He understands but he didn’t sign up for that. He says he has to go against his own team mate and now he has to worry about his own team mates going against each other. But something that he knows for a fact is that tomorrow, the young OG will show up, do his thing and represent for team USA. Cody thanks him and Je’Von walks away.

We cut to Byron Saxton who is backstage with Chelsea Green. She tells him that she doesn’t think Tiffany is obsessed with her Crown Jewel ring. She says she thinks Tiffany feels entitled about her SummerSlam moment and now she feels entitled about something that she never even earned. She says that ring is hers, she earned it and she will do whatever she has to do to get her ring back and Championship back.

Byron asks her about her match against Blake Monroe. Green says everywhere she goes, Blake is there but she is done being walked all over. She says last week, she slapped the glamour off her face and this week, she will finish the job.

-Commercial Break-

Blake Monroe vs. Chelsea Green

The bell rings and we are under way. Both women with right hands and they begin fighting on the outside of the ring. Green throws Monroe into the announce table and back into the ring. Green gets back into the ring and Monroe with a right hand. Green to the ropes but Green with a drop kick. Green with right hands but the referee breaks it up. Monroe with a kick to the face and a sling blade.

-Commercial Break-

Back from commercial, Green with a drop kick off the top rope. She goes for Unpretty Her but Monroe with a headbutt and a running knee to the face. We see Tiffany Stratton at ring side. Monroe with a back suplex. She runs towards Green but Green throws her into the turnbuckle. Green goes for the Unpretty Her but Tiffany gets o nthe ring apron. Green dslaps Tiffany and Green takes her Crown Jewel ring back. Monroe rolls Green but Green reverses the cover and gets the pin.

Winner: Chelsea Green

Tiffany hits Green from behind. Monroe hits Green and Tiffany grabs the ring from Green.

Paige’s music hits and out she comes to the ring. Paige with a drop kick onto Tiffany. Paige with a super kick onto Blake and she throws her to the outside of the ring.

We cut back to the Danhausen Thanksgiving. We see Giulia speaking Japanese to several WWE superstars and Hacksaw Jim Duggan. Ricky sees a gingerbread man costume and out comes Danhausen with a turkey and he says the turkey is done. Ricky says he doesn’t want turkey and he is not having fun. He looks outside and sees the grill is on fire. Danhausen hands Ricky the turkey but Ricky burns himself.

A promo of the MFTs airs and they talk about Bakusai.

-Commercial Break-

Back from commercial and out comes Trick Williams with Lil Yachty. Trick says he has to keep it real, he is pissed off. His knee is busted and now he can’t roll with Team USA for Survivor Series. He says all because of Damian Priest and because Priest can’t handle the truth. The one walking around weaving his head, eye shadow on, face full of makeup, looking like a late night side chick, thinking that he will disrespect Trick Williams, he must be stupid.

He says Damian is mad that he can’t handle the fact that Trick is getting all of these opportunities. He won’t apologize for being the Untied States Champion, for being the anointed one. He tells Priest that it’s not if, it’s when his knee gets better, when he is back to 100%, he will see him man to man, face to face, right here in the ring and he will knock him out and step on him with his lemon pepper steppers. His music hits and as he is about to leave, Damian Priest comes from behind. He hits Lil Yachty and he hits Trick onto his knee. Lil Yachty jumps onto Priest but Priest grabs him and throws him to the outside of the ring. Priest grabs Trick’s leg and he snaps Trick’s knee backwards.

We cut backstage and Sami Zayn is with Johnny Gargano and Candice LeRae. Zayn says he is not great, this whole Kevin situation is not great. He spoke to Nick Aldis, he wanted to talk to him about Gargano, that he deserves a title match but all Aldis kept talking about Kevin Owens. How he beat CM Punk and Gunther, how they have Championship implications. And now he has to team with Gunther and in comes Gunther.

He asks Zayn if Zayn can put his differences aside and Zayn says he can. Gunther says he meant every word he said to him last week and when it’s over, he is coming for the title. He tells Gargano and Le Rae to stay out of it. He walks away and Zayn tells them it’s fine.

-Commercial Break-
