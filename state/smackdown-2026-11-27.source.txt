WWE SmackDown Results
November 27, 2026
 Wichita, Kansas (Intrust Bank Arena) 
Commentary: Michael Cole and Corey Graves
Results by: Roy Nemer of Wrestleview.com

WWE SmackDown kicks off with clips of Raw where Cody Rhodes announced that Seth Rollins will be on Team USA at the Survivor Series.

In the arena, CM Punk’s music hits and out he comes to the ring. He talks about Thanksgiving and says that happy is not the word he is feeling right now. Punk says his genius captain Cody Rhodes picked Seth Rollins for his team. He says he is not pleased but what is a little shocking is that he is not mad. He says he is disappointed in his friend Cody, annoyed, incredibly. He says sometimes he forgets how much fun this business can be.

He says he had American socks on, his face painted, he was in the ring with Hacksaw Jim Duggan without the pressure of being Champion and now it feels like all of the air has been taken out of the room.

Cody’s music hits and out he comes to the ring.
