WWE SmackDown Results
November 27, 2026
 Wichita, Kansas (Intrust Bank Arena) 
Commentary: Michael Cole and Corey Graves
Results by: Roy Nemer of Wrestleview.com

WWE SmackDown kicks off with clips of Raw where Cody Rhodes announced that Seth Rollins will be on Team USA at the Survivor Series.

In the arena, CM Punk’s music hits and out he comes to the ring. He talks about Thanksgiving and says that happy is not the word he is feeling right now. Punk says his genius captain Cody Rhodes picked Seth Rollins for his team. He says he is not pleased but what is a little shocking is that he is not mad. He says he is disappointed in his friend Cody, annoyed, incredibly. He says sometimes he forgets how much fun this business can be.

He says he had American socks on, his face painted, he was in the ring with Hacksaw Jim Duggan without the pressure of being Champion and now it feels like all of the air has been taken out of the room.

Cody’s music hits and out he comes to the ring. Cody asks him if he wants him to apologize. Punk says he doesn’t have to apologize. Cody tells him that this is someone that revolutionized the business, one of the best of all time and someone who gives them a best time to win. Punk tells him he is crazy for saying that he revolutionized the business and he asks Cody if he is onm crack.

Punk says he wants to win but this is not worth it, this is not the price that he was willing to pay. Cody says for someone who taught him so much in his career why is he the one telling him that this doesn’t look like or sound like CM Punk of old. The CM Punk who holds a grudge for no reason. He says it is no secret that he is adored by the entire locker room, that one day he might run the show, it is no secret.

He says that is the CM Punk he loves and they know. He says he is not trying to make Punk feel better about the situation ,he knows that he has a problem with Seth Rollins. He says he doesn’t want Punk to be mad at Seth, he wants him to be mad at him. Punk says he is mad at Cody and he should be mad at himself for picking Seth. He tells him not to mention anything that Seth has done or said to him in the last 10 years. Punk says LA Knight is not thrilled, he thinks J’Veon maybe is happy to just be there. HE says it’s not just about him, out of the blue, Seth is suddenly patriotic. Punk says Seth is doing this not to win but for one reason and that is to piss him off.

Seth’s music hits and out he comes to the ring with a 2×4 stick. Seth welcomes everyone to the show. Seth says he has missed the fans, he missed being out in the ring, the energy, the burn it down. The fans start to sing his song and Seth asks them to sing it louder for CM Punk. The fans begin to sing. He says it feels good to be needed and to be needed by CM Punk.
